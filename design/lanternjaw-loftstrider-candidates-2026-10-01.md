# Lanternjaw and loftstrider: new candidates (2026-10-01)

Wrysk, 2026-10-01: "can you come up with like 4 new candidates for each one, using qwen
again to explore visually? get creative in the prose -- dont adhere to the existing

Exploration only; nothing here is picked. Images are in
`art/gen/runs/2026-10-01-lj-ls-candidates/`. The body plans ignore the dossiers and the
09-24 reimagining.

Only the ecology is kept, because the simulation and the predation design depend on it:

- **Lanternjaw.** Coyote scale: about 1.0 m long and 0.45 m tall (8 × 2 × 3.5 voxels).
  - It waits still, then strikes briefly.
  - It has a cyan lure whose glow costs reserve. Prey with a glow sense are drawn to it.
  - It eats grazers, shredders, capgnawers, grounded seedporters and newborn
    loftstriders. It also scavenges, and choristers shove it off carcasses.
  - It hatches from buried dormant eggs when something steps on them.
- **Loftstrider.** The largest animal, with its back about 1.6 m up when walking.
  - It eats foliage between 0.8 and 3.5 m: vaulttree shelves and drapes, umbrellafrond
    tiers, lanternberry. It drops much of it as litter.
  - Feeding is its braced, vulnerable posture, which a chorister pack exploits.
  - It kicks in defence.
  - One young follows the parent.
  - It has two sense sites, one for the canopy and one for the route.

## Lanternjaw

### LJ-1 · Censer (scorpid)

A lacquered scorpid that carries its lantern in a cage made of its own tail.

**Body.**
- **Build:** low, broad and flat, like a dropped shield.
- **Pincers:** two great flat pincers, held closed side by side in front of the face like
  a pair of shut doors. Pale comb teeth line their inner faces and show only when the
  doors swing open.
- **Head:** a short head behind the doors, with a row of four small magenta eye pits.
- **Legs:** six short splayed legs in three pairs.
- **Tail:** five thick segments rise from the rear and curl forward over the back. The
  end is not a sting but a hollow lattice of four curved ribs around a glowing cyan bead.
  This lantern cage hangs just above and ahead of the doors.
- **Colour:** lacquered blue-black with a violet sheen. The leg joints and pincer hinges
  are rusty orange, the one warm note.

**Hunt.** It sits in the litter with the cage lit. Prey that comes to the light walks
into the doors, which snap shut sideways. To hide, it closes the ribs over the bead. The
paid glow is a shutter, not a switch, so it dims rather than going dark.

**Qwen.** One lanternjaw, a low flat scorpion-like ambush predator, its head at the right
edge of the frame. At the front, two broad flat pincers are held closed side by side
before the face like a pair of shut doors, their hinges rusty orange. Behind them a short
head carries a row of four small magenta eye pits. The body is a wide flat lacquered
blue-black carapace with a violet sheen, resting low on six short splayed legs in three
pairs with rusty orange joints. From the rear, a thick tail of five segments curls up and
forward over the back; its tip is an open cage of four curved ribs around a glowing cyan
bead, hanging just above and ahead of the closed pincers. Still waiting pose, feet planted.

### LJ-2 · Tripwire (scorpid)

A flattened scorpid that sets its own pincers as a spring trap.

**Body.**
- **Back:** one broad oval plate pressed to the ground. It is textured like weathered
  stone and speckled with teal lichen and pale crust, so at rest it reads as a rock.
- **Pincers:** two very long, thin pincer arms lie flat on the ground ahead of it. They
  are spread in a wide V with the scissor jaws cocked open, like a trap-jaw ant's.
  - Three pale trigger hairs stand up between the arms.
  - The pincers are pale lilac inside, with magenta tips.
- **Tail:** slender and jointed. It rises from the rear like a fishing rod and bends
  forward over the body. A fine glowing cyan thread hangs from its tip, with a bead at the
  end dangling just above the trigger hairs.
- **Legs:** eight short legs, tucked under the plate's rim.
- **Eggs:** a row of small pearl-blue egg capsules sits along the plate's rim. This is the
  egg bank, carried until it is buried.

**Hunt.** Prey that noses the bead brushes the hairs, and the scissors slam shut.

**Qwen.** One lanternjaw, a flattened scorpion-like trap predator, its head at the right
edge of the frame. Two very long thin pincer arms lie flat on the ground ahead of it,
spread wide apart in a V, their scissor jaws held open, pale lilac inside with magenta
tips; three pale trigger hairs stand up from the ground between them. Behind them, the
body is one broad oval back plate pressed flat to the ground, grey-violet like weathered
stone, speckled with teal lichen and pale crust, with a row of small pearl-blue egg
capsules along its rim. Eight short legs tuck under the plate's edge. From the rear, a
slender jointed tail rises and arches forward like a fishing rod; from its tip a fine
glowing cyan thread hangs down, ending in a small glowing cyan bead dangling just above
the trigger hairs.

### LJ-3 · Deadfall (mimic)

A piece of fallen vaulttree with a glowcap growing on it, until it bites.

**Body.**
- **Shape:** long and low, like a split section of fallen log.
- **Bark:** ridged dark violet bark plates with teal moss in the cracks.
- **Jaws:** the two flanks are long bark-plated jaw halves. They meet along the top in a
  jagged seam that runs the whole length of the animal.
- **Lure:** a glowcap grows from the seam near the front. It has a short pale stalk and a
  purple cap with flat dark spots, glowing cyan at the brim and underside.
- **Eyes:** two magenta knots in the bark at the front end.
- **Legs:** four short thick stumps, mostly hidden, like broken branch stubs.

**Hunt.** Everything comes to glowing food, and whatever eats the cap is standing on the
seam. The log splits open along its length like a book, showing a cavernous magenta
interior lined with pale pegs.

**Sim note.** The lure no longer sits at the tail tip, so the simulation's glow source
would move to the front of the back.

**Qwen.** One lanternjaw that looks like a split section of fallen tree trunk lying on
the ground, about four times as long as it is tall, its front end at the right edge of
the frame. Its back and sides are ridged dark violet bark plates with teal moss in the
cracks; a jagged seam runs along the top of the whole log, where the two bark halves
meet. Near the front end a small mushroom grows from the seam: a short pale stalk and a
purple cap with flat dark spots, its brim and underside glowing cyan. At the front end
two magenta eyes sit in bark knots, and the seam is parted a little, showing a dark
magenta mouth lined with pale pegs. Four short thick stump legs, like broken branch
stubs, hold it just off the ground.

### LJ-4 · Open Lantern (gaper)

It waits with its mouth open, and the light is inside.

**Body.**
- **Build:** short, heavy and low, on four bowed, wide-set legs.
- **Head:** almost all of the front is a huge head that is mostly jaw, with a broad flat
  skull and a shovel-shaped underjaw.
- **Lure:** it waits with the mouth gaping nearly 90° open. A fat cyan tongue-bulb lies
  on the floor of the mouth. Its light shows pale lilac gums and a magenta throat and
  spills onto the ground in front.
- **Eyes:** small, high and far back on the skull.
- **Hide:** dusky slate blue with darker mottles. A ridge of short blunt plates runs down
  the spine to a short thick tail.
- **Cheek windows:** translucent, one on each side. They glow cyan when the mouth is
  shut, so the lantern is seen through the jaw.

**Hunt.** Prey comes to the light and steps in, and the jaw closes. As it swallows, the
light behind the cheek windows dims.

**Qwen.** One lanternjaw, a short heavy low ambush predator on four bowed wide-set legs,
its head at the right edge of the frame. Most of the front is an enormous head that is
mostly jaw: a broad flat skull and a shovel-shaped underjaw, held wide open. Lying on the
floor of the open mouth is a fat glowing cyan tongue bulb that lights the inside of the
mouth: pale lilac gums, a magenta throat, cyan light falling on the ground in front. Two
small eyes sit high and far back on the skull. The hide is dusky slate blue with darker
mottles; a ridge of short blunt plates runs down the spine to a short thick tail. Waiting
pose, body low, the open underjaw resting on the ground.

## Loftstrider

Each candidate has two prompts:
- **Feeding**, on a tall canvas. This is its silhouette and its weak moment.
- **Walking**, on a wide canvas.

### LS-H · Uncoiler

A tall, plush, moth-like browser that eats with a proboscis it unrolls into the canopy.

**Body.**
- **Build:** a deep rounded body on four long jointed legs. The shoulders are high and
  slope to a low rump.
- **Fur:** dense deep blue, with a pale violet belly.
- **Plumes:** two huge feathery antenna plumes, saffron-gold, sweep back from the head
  over the shoulders. They are its canopy sense.
- **Eyes:** two dark round eyes, for the route.
- **Proboscis:** below the eyes, rolled into a flat watch-spring spiral as big as the
  head. It is lilac banded with violet.
- **Defence:** a hooked spur on each hind ankle.

**Feeding.** It plants its legs wide and uncoils the proboscis straight up two metres into
the crown. The tip is a magenta rasp rosette that scours the foliage, and shredded leaf
drifts down all around. With its head locked upward, it cannot see a pack coming.

**Qwen, feeding.** One loftstrider, a tall plush alien browser, feeding under the dark
teal foliage shelf of a tall tree at the top of the frame. A deep rounded body with dense
deep-blue fur and a pale violet belly stands high on four long jointed legs planted wide,
its high shoulders sloping to a low rump. Two large feathery saffron-gold antenna plumes
sweep back from the head over the shoulders. Below two dark round eyes, a long lilac
proboscis banded with violet rises straight up from the face into the foliage, three
times the height of the body, its tip a magenta rasping rosette; shredded blue leaf
fragments drift down around it. A hooked spur on each hind ankle.

**Qwen, walking.** One loftstrider walking, a tall plush alien browser, its head at the
right edge of the frame. A deep rounded body with dense deep-blue fur and a pale violet
belly is carried high on four long jointed legs, its high shoulders sloping to a low
rump. Two large feathery saffron-gold antenna plumes sweep back from the head over the
shoulders. Below two dark round eyes, a lilac proboscis banded with violet is rolled into
a tight flat spiral, like a watch spring as large as the head. A hooked spur on each hind
ankle.

### LS-I · Hoist

A heavy crested browser that jacks itself up into the canopy on locking legs and rakes
the canopy with its back.

**Body.**
- **Hull:** long and keeled, deep teal, with dark indigo plates on the flanks.
- **Crest:** two rows of tall curved comb plates run down the spine. They are bone-lilac,
  with glowing cyan edges and fine teeth along their front edges.
- **Legs:** four legs of three long segments each. Walking, they are folded in deep Z
  shapes, like a carpenter's folding rule.
- **Head:** small and low at the front, on a short neck, with two magenta route eyes.
- **Canopy sense:** a band of magenta patches along the base of the crest looks up.

**Feeding.** It walks under a canopy shelf and unfolds all four legs straight, lifting
its body until the crest rakes up through the shelf. It rocks forward and back to shred.
Fragments slide down grooves in the crest to the mouth at the shoulders, and much of the
leaf falls. With its legs locked straight it cannot run until it folds down, and the
pack goes for the legs.

**Qwen, feeding.** One loftstrider, a heavy alien browser, feeding under a dark teal
canopy shelf at the top of the frame. Four long legs, each of three straight segments,
stand fully unfolded and locked straight, lifting a long keeled deep teal body high so
the crest on its back pushes up into the foliage. The crest is two rows of tall curved
comb plates running the length of the spine, bone-lilac with glowing cyan edges and fine
teeth on their front edges; shredded leaves fall from them to the ground. A small low
head at the front on a short neck has two magenta eyes; a band of magenta patches runs
along the base of the crest. Dark indigo armour plates on the flanks, violet leg joints.

**Qwen, walking.** One loftstrider walking, a heavy alien browser, its head at the right
edge of the frame. A long keeled deep teal body with dark indigo armour plates on the
flanks is held level on four long legs, each folded in a deep zigzag of three segments
like a carpenter's folding rule, violet at the joints. Along the spine runs a crest of
two rows of tall curved comb plates, bone-lilac with glowing cyan edges and fine teeth on
their front edges, with a band of magenta patches along their base. A small low head on a
short neck at the front has two magenta eyes.

### LS-J · Periscope

Two heads: a low one that watches the ground, and a long one that grows from its back to
eat the sky.

**Body.**
- **Build:** massive, barrel-shaped and level, on six thick legs. The hind pair is
  spurred.
- **Hide:** leathery plum, with paler lilac stripes.
- **Route head:** low and wide at the front, with a broad snout, two big magenta eyes and
  two small scraping tusks. It smells and watches.
- **Feeding stalk:** rises from the middle of the back. It is a long, flexible, muscular
  neck with no eyes, only a ring of cyan light-pits under a narrow scissor beak. At rest
  it lies folded back along the spine to the tail, like a lowered mast on a deck.

**Feeding.** The stalk rises straight up into the canopy and clips, while the front head
keeps watch along the ground. It is the one browser that can't be surprised from in
front, but its flanks and six legs are open to a pack from behind.

**Qwen, feeding.** One loftstrider, a massive barrel-bodied six-legged alien browser, its
low head at the right edge of the frame, feeding under a dark teal canopy shelf at the
top of the frame. The body is level, leathery plum hide with paler lilac stripes, on six

**Qwen, walking.** One loftstrider walking, a massive barrel-bodied six-legged alien

### LS-K · Anemone

A walking dome whose crown of feeding tentacles blooms upward into the canopy.

**Body.**
- **Dome:** a big smooth domed body like an overturned bowl, 1.5 m across. It is pale
  lilac-pink, mottled with darker violet rings.
- **Legs:** four thick columnar legs, deep plum, with broad round feet.
- **Route sense:** a ring of magenta eye patches around the dome's rim.
- **Tentacles:** a crown of about twenty, deep violet with glowing cyan tips, rising from
  a ring-shaped opening on top. The cyan tips are its canopy sense. Walking, they are
  drawn into a tight bud on top of the dome.

**Feeding.** The crown blooms upward like a sea anemone. The tentacles stretch to the
canopy, wrap fronds and pull them down into the central mouth, and leaves shower down the
dome's sides.

**Qwen, feeding.** One loftstrider, an alien browser shaped like a walking dome, feeding
under a dark teal canopy shelf at the top of the frame. A big smooth domed body like an
overturned bowl, pale lilac-pink with darker violet ring mottles, stands on four thick
columnar deep plum legs with broad round feet. A ring of magenta eye patches circles the
dome's rim. From an opening in the top of the dome, a crown of about twenty long deep
violet tentacles with glowing cyan tips rises like a sea anemone in bloom, stretching up

**Qwen, walking.** One loftstrider walking, an alien browser shaped like a walking dome,
