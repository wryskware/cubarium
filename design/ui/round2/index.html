Three type roles: Title (species names, the big population number), Text (names in lists, the field note, facts, chips) and Chrome (tabs, labels, the clock, keycaps, axis labels). "Chrome fonts" shows every chrome candidate with your title and text picks; "Full screen" shows all three on the Lanternjaw page. Fonts with a pixel grid are drawn at whole multiples of it, so Size steps one multiple. Variable fonts get a slider per axis; the line under the sliders is the CSS to send back. Numbers are sample data. Settings stay in this browser.
