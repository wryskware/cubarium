## Why

`design/7_Research/training-review-2026-10-14.md` (merged 6d75da0d): an ES step is ~1% signal at
1024 twin pairs; a mostly-signal step costs hours. Its §4 option D, which Wrysk approved as a
pilot (2026-10-14, "sure pilot it"): during training the top **samples** its intent proposal at
the arbitration temperature (hold and margin stay environment rules); each decision gets an
advantage = discounted return over the next minutes minus a learned value baseline (GAE); the
update is the advantage-weighted log-prob gradient of the top. Stage 1: the bottom is frozen, so
the top's input is fixed and no backprop through time is needed — reuse `two_layer/clone.rs`'s
top cross-entropy gradient (~:924, finite-difference tested `tests.rs` ~:718) with weights.
Data come from the live windows; no twin copies (~25× less sim per generation).

Wrysk on the score: "i dont think 'how much food it ate' is the right optimization function,
not in itself anyway … survival and even … happiness (curiosity/exploration) … we need richer
scores". A separate research package decides the score. **This pilot uses today's score**
(net energy incl. the loss at death, plus kin shares per `design/behaviour-ai-2026-10-11.md`
§10.3 — per body, which PG gives directly) **behind a per-decision reward interface** that can
take more terms later (e.g. the existing novelty term, `config/training/curiosity.toml`) without
touching the learner.

## Do

1. **Decision log** in live windows for the trained lineage: per body per top decision, what
   the top saw (its input/hidden state as the clone uses it), the menu mask, the sampled intent
   and its log-prob, time; per body a reward stream (the score's terms per interval).
2. **Learner:** returns with a discount (knob), a value head (on the top's features, trained by
   regression), GAE (λ knob), PPO-style clipped update (clip knob), entropy bonus (knob, small),
   Adam. Every number a serde knob in the training config with a doc comment (training-side, like
   `kin-twins-2026-10-13.toml`; not FaunaConfig). Deploy/check at argmax as today.
3. **Tests (write them before the learner; mechanism only, no tuned numbers):** on a tiny
   fixture/bandit where one intent pays more, updates raise its probability; a negative advantage
   lowers it; the gradient matches finite differences; the value head's regression reduces its
   error; a masked intent never gets probability; the plain path and ES are untouched
   (`a_plain_lineage_trains_exactly_as_today` passes; ES twin runs unchanged).
4. **Yardstick:** the review's split-half cosine — form one generation's update from two halves
   of its bodies/worlds and compare (`crates/cubarium-search/examples/step_noise.rs` does it for
   ES). Run the pilot on the lanternjaw from its clone centre with **24 check seeds**, next to an
   ES all-sign twin run with the same check seeds, both ~3 h, submitted (not quick). Report split
   cosine per generation, check gains brain-vs-rules, diet readout (CHECK-DIET), and what the
   behaviour readouts show (switches, scene agreement) — watch for the seedporter's "sit still"
   failure. Use more desktop threads for both if free (no synthetic load).
