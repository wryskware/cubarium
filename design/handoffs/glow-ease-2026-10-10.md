# GLOW-EASE: glowing things never jump; lanterns flicker, flowers glow by nectar

Rules: `design/handoffs/worker-rules-2026-10-07.md` (no windows, 5-minute calls, own
`target/` seeded by reflink from main's, explicit-path commits, MP4 recipe, ≤ 40-line
return). No `unsafe` except the approved `ash` FFI calls inside `cubarium-gpu`, one call per
block. Worktrees and targets never go under `/tmp` (a RAM disk). Put every video you hand
back under the **main checkout's** `art/gen/runs/glow-ease-2026-10-10/` (absolute path),
not inside the worktree: ignored files die with it. Recompile shaders with
`crates/cubarium-gpu/shaders/compile.sh` and commit the .spv.

## Why

Wrysk (2026-10-10, his display world at night, desktop, lit tier): "i actually figured out
finally what the light stepping was. its the vine flowers coming on or off at night, and
same with lantern berries. glowy things should never change brightness discontinuously, and
we should add some flicker to them. lanternberries could flicker subtley like candles.
latticevine flowers can cycle their glow on/off throughout the night (although i thought it
was supposed to glow proportional to how much nectar they have, which in any case is great
for being a continuous value then)"

Today emission is a yes/no per cell (`crates/cubarium/src/voxel/colours.rs`
`plant_emission`, `vine_emission`): a lanternberry's lantern glows while `ripe`, a
latticevine flower's bell mouth glows while the accent is there, a glowcap lip steps
None → Fruiting → Ripe, a bloomcrown's bloom swaps blush → orange when ripe. Each switch is
a step in the cell, in the light it throws into the glow volume, and in the bloom. The
glowcap candle (`crates/cubarium/src/voxel/candle.rs`: 16 curve classes, each its own copy
of the lip style, because styles are scarce, `cubarium_gpu::voxel::MAX_STYLES`) is the one
precedent for animated emission.

## 1. The fix (ships without asking): no emitter ever steps

Find every way an emitting texel's brightness or colour can change between two frames in a
step, from evidence (a night capture, per-frame luminance of the emitting cells and of the
lit area around them), not guesses. At least: the four switches above; the glow volume and
the bloom they feed (check whether the glow volume's light updates only on a rebake or a
pack, e.g. `[light] volumetric_rebake_s`, and cross-fade it if so); the vine's accent
appearing and going.

Make every emitter's brightness continuous on the frame clock: a switch eases over a fade
(a `[light]` knob in seconds, a subtle first guess, stated), and a colour swap
cross-fades. Brightness must stay continuous, not quantised into visible rungs: if you carry
brightness through style classes like the candle, show the rung size is below a visible
step, or carry a per-stand or per-cell intensity the shader reads instead. Your call;
state why.

When the cell itself goes (a lantern eaten or dropped, a flower eaten), at least the light it
threw into its surroundings and its bloom must ease out; say whether the cell's own texels
can fade too and what it would cost. A cell vanishing as geometry is the same pop every
eaten thing has today; report it, don't redesign eating.

## 2. Looks Wrysk asked for

- **Lanternberry candle flicker (on by default).** Each lantern's glow flickers subtly like
  a candle: smooth band-limited noise (no frame-to-frame white noise; it must not read as
  stepping), each lantern or plant on its own phase. `[light]` knobs for its depth and
  speed, subtle first guesses, stated. He tunes from video.
- **Latticevine flowers glow by nectar (on by default).** A flower's brightness follows its
  vine's nectar fill, continuously (the fauna side already dims a drained flower for the
  animals' eyes: `crates/cubarium-voxel-fauna/src/flower_fruit_light_tests.rs`,
  `brightness_seen`; use the same fill, don't invent a second one). Measure how much the
  fill actually moves over a night in a real world (World 2 or a fresh one, a short run)
  and state it.
- **Flower cycle (candidate, a switch, off by default).** Wrysk's other idea: each flower's
  glow slowly cycles on and off through the night, on its own phase, times its nectar
  glow. Build it so he can compare it with nectar alone.

Keep the other emitters' looks (glowcap candle, bloomcrown) as they are apart from the
fix. Don't invent other looks.

## Tests (write these first; mechanisms, no tuned numbers)

- An emitter switched on (or off) reaches its new brightness only through intermediate
  values over frames, and never moves more in one frame than the fade allows.
- A colour swap passes through blends of the two colours.
- A flower's brightness rises with its nectar fill and is 0 only when empty or by day as
  today.
- The flicker is continuous (its frame-to-frame change is a small share of its depth at 60
  fps) and its long mean stays near the unflickered brightness.
