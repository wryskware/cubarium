# 02 — Genuine quiet habits

Outcome: occasional readable calm that follows real, affordable behavior, balanced
with care activity. Calm fin art is already shipped; it is not proof of resting biology.

The birth-only recovery candidate occupied about 0.03% of living time in its
reviewed two-hour study. It was closed as too sparse, not proven to cause ecosystem
collapse. The live rest policy remains unchanged. Post-intake shadow instrumentation
exists only in isolated source; it measures opportunities without changing behavior.

## First bounded milestone

Review the final shadow implementation at
`checkpoint/source-post-intake-shadow-2026-09-13` against its four saved public-API
counterexample probes. Reconcile which issues are already fixed before proposing
any change: baseline inactive-mode admission, censored event reconciliation,
dying-member exposure at a completed boundary, and known-form attribution.
Do not assert that every old probe still fails at the final checkpoint.

Either close that small instrumentation review with focused tests or explain why
the harness is not worth carrying forward. No behavioral rollout or broad cohort
is part of this first milestone. If useful, the next separately bounded question
is how often actual intake leaves enough reserve and opportunity for a visible pause.
