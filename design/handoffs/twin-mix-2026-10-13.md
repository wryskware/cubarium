## Why

Live training now uses all-sign twins (Wrysk 2026-10-13: "accept twin world training";
`kin/twins.rs`, merged 71edea40). A twin's score is the **sum over every body of the lineage**
in the world, and every body carries the same sign. That rewards a change that helps the
species as a whole even at the carrier's own cost: kindness to unrelated conspecifics.
Topic 2 (Wrysk's call) is own energy plus a share of relatives' (kin shares). Wrysk asked for
"a hybrid score"; the agreed form:

- In each twin, a random subset of the lineage (`carrier_share`, e.g. 0.5) carries `±ε_p`; the
  rest run the centre, in both copies alike (same subset in both copies).
- Carriers' differences = direct effect on self + social effect from the other carriers.
  Non-carriers' differences = social effect from the carriers only.
- From both, estimate the per-carrier **direct** effect and the **social** effect on others,
  split the social part into relatives (kin shares, as topic 2) and strangers, and score
  `direct + kin-weighted relatives + stranger_weight × strangers`.
- `stranger_weight` default **0** (topic 2 exactly). `carrier_share = 1, stranger_weight = 1`
  must reproduce today's all-sign twins byte-identically.

Derive the estimator yourself and write it in the doc comment (what each term estimates,
its bias when carriers and non-carriers differ, why the subset is drawn per twin).
LJ-TWIN's finding to keep in mind: a focal flip (one carrier) has ICC ~0 because it reshuffles
neighbours' luck; all-sign has ICC ~0.1. Half carriers should sit between; measure where.

## Do

1. Knobs on `TwinKnobs` (`kin/config.rs`): `carrier_share`, `stranger_weight` (serde, doc
   comments, registry rows). Defaults: carrier_share 0.5, stranger_weight 0. Note: this
   changes the twins mode's default score; that is intended (Wrysk's kin call); the running
   stage-1 jobs use a pinned binary and are unaffected.
2. The carrier draw (deterministic from the twin's seed), the per-body metering split
   carrier / non-carrier / relative-of-carrier, the estimator, and the update via
   `ascend_pairs` as today. Readouts per generation: direct and social parts, carriers per
   twin, null-twin gap.
3. Tests (mechanism, small fixtures, a few hundred ticks at most):
   - null table → identical twins, zero update;
   - a scene where `+` helps only the carrier → positive at any dial;
   - a scene where `+` helps others at the carrier's cost → negative at dial 0, positive at
     dial 1 with enough benefit;
   - `carrier_share = 1, stranger_weight = 1` byte-identical to the all-sign path;
   - a `per_body` lineage in the same run unchanged; `a_plain_lineage_trains_exactly_as_today`.
4. **Signal probe** on the lanternjaw, σ 0.05, 2-minute windows, from live starts
   (`--top-up lanternjaw=8:0.3`), like LJ-TWIN's (`examples/lj_twin.rs`,
   `scripts/brain-pilot/twin_signal.py`): ICC of the hybrid score (dial 0, carriers 0.5)
   against all-sign on the same starts, and the thread-hours per update for a cosine of 0.2
   with the gradient. Submit it as a job if it doesn't fit the quick lane; hand back.
5. Specs (not submitted) for LJ and SP stage-1 on the hybrid, like
   `config/lab/specs/live-twins-{lj,sp}-stage1.toml`, priced.
