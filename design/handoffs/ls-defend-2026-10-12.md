# LS-DEFEND: calves run to their mothers, adults bunch, face out and kick

## Rules

Read `design/handoffs/behaviour-ai-worker-rules-2026-10-12.md` first. It covers windows, the
5-minute limit, labd, builds, tests, knobs, saves, commits and the return format. Use graft
(`graft ask … --source`, `graft callers`) before grepping.

**One exception to the rules file's "don't change an existing default":** this is Wrysk's
decided behaviour (§0 rows 6, 7, 8 and his ask at the top), so it ships **on by default** for
the loftstrider. Other lineages stay as today.

## Why

This is package 4 of `design/behaviour-ai-2026-10-11.md`. Read:
- §0, all of it: his ask is the brief. In his words: "giraffe or elephant defense … multiple
  adults together can generally defend themselves. lone juveniles are the most defenseless.
  they should generally run to the adults … and adults alert and run to defend the babies";
- all of §3;
- §7's loftstrider law tests;
- §11.4 and §11.6.

The ethology is in `design/7_Research/behaviour-ai-ethology-2026-10-11.md`. One fact there:
moose that stood their ground were killed 0 of 36 times; those that ran, 5 of 41.

**Today's baseline** (`design/7_Research/engage-baseline-2026-10-12.md`, 450 staged
episodes): two to four Veilhounds starting 10–14 m out kill a grown adult in 78–96 % of
encounters, median 17–41 s. They never give up, and are never hurt.

**His target** (topic 8, §6):
- about one loftstrider a world-year, mostly strayed calves;
- an adult once in several years;
- a standoff every 1–2 h that usually ends with the pack giving up;
- a hound killed by kicks about once in a few years.

**Already merged:**
- CONTRACTS (§11): menus and held-intent records, typed subjects, the kick state machine
  (off by default), `RestAsk`, call records.
- CALLS-REST:
  - loftstrider snort and bleat with the mother-first response;
  - light and deep rest, and waking;
  - `kin_tracks` 4 and the "mine" mark.
  - The parent merges it before you start. If your base lacks `calls.rs` and `sleep.rs`,
    say so at once.
- The engagement harness: `cubarium_voxel_sim::engagement`.
- VH-TACTICS runs in parallel (branch `vh-tactics`): risk-aware target choice reading
  guard stance and facing, giving up, strike identity, and bodies blocking lunges.

**Ships:** calves run to their mothers, adults bunch, face out and kick.

## Checks (written into tests as you go; §7)

- A calf with a pressing threat moves toward its mother if bonded, otherwise the nearest
  adult, and settles on the far side.
- An adult hearing a distress call moves to the guard point between the calf and the hound.
- A kick lands only after the windup, on a hound in reach and arc, then recovers. A
  kick-back during recovery is refused, so there's one budget.
- A dodged strike draws no counter.
- Three landed kicks in one fight kill a grown hound at the configured damage. Read the
  numbers from the config; don't pin them.
- Two adults facing two hounds from different sectors don't both face the same one.
- Dispersal still happens with all-age herding on.
