# 03 — A viable Lanternjaw lineage

Wrysk selected Fable's Lanternjaw body. Keep it; this is not another body contest.
The multipart rig and world adapter exist in main, but no hunters are enabled in
the live world. Rarity around one or two was the owner's desired feel, not a
license to add a population quota or automatic replacement.

The size-aware charging/growth gate produced paid juveniles but did not establish
adult recruitment. The saved full-cohort interpretation reports 15 offspring,
all short of adult size and subsequently starved; an adverse prey loss also matters.
That interpretation was checkpointed, not accepted as a successful candidate.
Successful process exit, accounting parity and attractive adult art are insufficient.

## First bounded milestone

Read the saved biological review and growth proposal, then inspect **one** likely
acquisition/allocation bottleneck between juvenile intake and maturity. Separate
what compact reports support from claims requiring deleted raw histories. Define
one testable mechanism and its falsifying result before adding another candidate.
Do not rerun the entire six-arm cohort to get started.
