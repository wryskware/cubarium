# VH-TEACH: why the Veilhound clone can't tell rest from den, or search from the hunt

  (`quick = true`, `hosts = ["desktop"]`, `cwd` set, under 15 minutes). A full-size clone is

## Why

The two-layer Veilhound clone agrees poorly with its rules teacher. Full 8-seed clone on main
1a205be7: `runs/lab/1013-085701-vh-full-cov8-reclone-6d45/run` (spec
`runs/lst-specs/vh-full-cov8-1a205be7.toml`, binary `runs/bin/two_layer_clone-1a205be7`;
`stdout.log` has the held-out confusion, `run/heldout_picks.jsonl` the picks). Held-out:
- **rest .17**: 1312 of 1576 rest labels picked as `den`;
- **search .53**: confused with test (403), drive (488), feed (413);
- stalk .36, intercept 0 (19 labels); drive .82, give_up .87, feed 1.00, den .95.
An earlier read said the rules' rest→den choice ignores hunger. The LS clone had the same
kind of trouble and LS-TEACH / LS-MOVEOFF fixed it (see `design/handoffs/ls-teach-2026-10-13.md`,
merges 067eb4be, 3c2e7058): labels that named something the senses couldn't carry, or that
misnamed what the body did.

## Do

1. **Diagnose rest vs den** (file:line): what in the rules picks rest vs den, and what the clone's
   senses (`manifest.rs`, the VH obs channels) carry of it. Possible causes: the two differ only
   by state the hound can't sense (pack bookkeeping, a den flag); the label names one while the
   body does the other (resting at the den labelled rest); the distinction is hunger the clone
   can't read. Use `mask_trace` (detail/top/dump) as LS-MOVEOFF did.
2. **Diagnose search vs test/drive/feed** the same way (sample label rows and what the body did).
3. **Fix** where a label misnames what the body does (relabel), or expose a sense the animal
   plausibly has (manifest pattern, as CLONE-AGREE did for `Rhythm`). If the rules read
   something the hound can't sense, say so and propose; don't change heuristic behaviour.
4. **Re-measure** with a small quick-lane clone (`confusion=1`) before/after, same seeds.
5. **Hand back** a full-size spec like `runs/lst-specs/vh-full-cov8-1a205be7.toml` on your binary,
   submitted (it queues), with its job id.
