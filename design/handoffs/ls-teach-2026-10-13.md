# LS-TEACH: loftstrider teacher worlds with calves and hounds, then re-clone

## Rules

Read `design/handoffs/behaviour-ai-worker-rules-2026-10-12.md` first (the 2026-10-12 rules hold).
- Never wrap `scripts/lab/lab` in `timeout`. Quick lane only for what you run yourself
  (`quick = true`, `hosts = ["desktop"]`, `cwd` set, under 15 minutes). Full-size clones are
  submitted (not quick) and handed back; never wait on a job.
- The desktop is busy with brain training until ~06:00; eidolon has calf batches until ~06:30.
  Submitted jobs simply queue; that's fine.
- Build in your own worktree's `target/` (reflink-seeded) at -j 8 or lower. Job binaries go to
  the main checkout's `runs/bin/<name>-<commit>`.

## Why

The two-layer loftstrider clone agrees poorly with its rules teacher (CLONE-AGREE, merged
8069d45f; brief `design/handoffs/clone-agree-2026-10-12.md`). Full 8-seed clone
`runs/lab/1013-002926-ca-full-cov8-*` (loftstrider): graze .77, guard .94, rest .54,
**watch .23, move_off .22**. Findings:
- 981 of 1015 training `watch` labels are masked: the label names a threat the clone's
  senses don't carry as a track (no threat in sight), so it can't be learned.
- The family intents (stay_with_family, go_to_calf, go_to_adults) and explore never appear:
  the teacher worlds have no calves (a calf is born only after ~7 h of reserve).
- Rest is confused with watch (a full loftstrider standing still).

New since: the scenario file's `[[calve]]` (CALF-PLACE, merged 61e3dc8c;
`crates/cubarium-voxel-sim/src/edit.rs` `Edit::calve`, scenario `CalveEntry`) places calves
with their mother and an age on any world, and `[[place]]` / `[[populate]]` place hounds.

## Do

1. **Find how the teacher worlds are built** for `two_layer_clone`
   (`crates/cubarium-search/examples/two_layer_clone.rs`, `train_seeds`, the label trace) and
   give them a scenario: calves for the family (`[[calve]]`, ages spread over the straying
   range), and Veilhounds placed at sight range of the family often enough that threat
   labels occur with the threat in the loftstrider's senses. Use the same teacher (rules)
   and labels; change only the worlds. A switch that defaults off, or a scenario path arg.
2. **The masked watch labels:** confirm why they are masked (file:line). If the label fires
   from state the animal plausibly senses (a threat it heard, a packmate's alarm), expose it
   as a sense following the manifest pattern (`manifest.rs`, as CLONE-AGREE did for
   `Rhythm`); if it reads something the animal cannot sense, say so and propose. Labels change
   only where they misname what the body does.
3. **Re-measure** with a small quick-lane clone (confusion matrix, `confusion=1`) before and
   after, same seeds, and report per-intent agreement including the family intents.
4. **Hand back** a full-size 8-seed loftstrider clone spec on your binary (like the
   `ca-full-cov8` spec), submitted (it queues), with its job id.
