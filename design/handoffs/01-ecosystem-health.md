# 01 — Ordinary ecosystem health

Outcome: an unattended world with understandable plant recovery and viable ordinary
fauna replacement. Do not equate high total population with diversity or health.

The old live world collapsed to two kinds and looked nearly plantless. It has
been reset, not repaired. Historical skimmer losses followed initial growth;
reviewed development-flow pilots show some descendants maturing before recruitment
stops. Funding-site success does not rule out controller eligibility problems.
The remaining ten replays finished, but their raw outputs were deleted before
completed interpretation. Do not resume their old reduction commands.

## First bounded milestone

Take a small read-only census of the current owner: per-kind population, producer
distribution and how that distribution selects visible plant stages. Determine
whether bare-looking flora reflects depleted producers, presentation thresholds,
or both. This is a diagnostic observation, not a before/after experiment.

Then choose **one** causal question about ordinary replacement, preferably from
the existing reviewed pilots. Inspect the relevant controller/intake/allocation
path and produce one focused regression probe or short seeded diagnostic. Report
what the evidence rules out and what it does not. Do not start a multi-day or
12-seed sweep as the opening move.

free offspring, routine reseeding, or stronger care dependency to conceal decline.
Keep hunter introduction and broad autonomy tuning out of this slice.
