# LJ-TWIN: does a top-layer variant change a lanternjaw's window at all?

## Rules

Read `design/handoffs/behaviour-ai-worker-rules-2026-10-12.md` first (the 2026-10-12 rules hold).
- Never wrap `scripts/lab/lab` in `timeout`. Quick lane only for what you run (`quick = true`,
  `hosts = ["desktop"]`, `cwd` set, under 15 minutes). For a longer run, write the spec,
  submit it, and hand back with the job id. Never wait on it.
- Build in your own worktree's `target/` (reflink-seeded) at -j 8 or lower. Job binaries go to
  the main checkout's `runs/bin/<name>-<commit>`.

## Why

LJ-SIGNAL (merged 0750c24c; `scripts/brain-pilot/pair_signal.py`, `examples/live_replay.rs`)
measured the variant's share of a within-world pair difference (ICC) in live 10-minute training
windows. The probe `runs/lab/1013-002625-ljsig-lj-probe-dd38/run/summary.txt` held the
learning rate at 0, used 8 lanternjaws per world, 16 worlds × 16 pairs × 20 generations, and
4 repeats per pair. Results by σ:

| σ | ICC | 95 % | sd of pair difference |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 0.002 | [-0.042, 0.050] | 1.09 |
| 0.02 | -0.013 | [-0.055, 0.034] | 0.99 |
| 0.05 | -0.054 | [-0.092, -0.011] | 1.14 |
| 0.1 | -0.022 | [-0.062, 0.022] | 1.19 |

Even at σ 0.1 (the top's weights have rms ~0.23), the same variant doesn't do consistently
better or worse across worlds. I checked that one perturbation table per generation is shared
by every world (`trainer.rs` `tables_for`; candidates `2p`/`2p+1`), so the repeats are true
repeats.

Two explanations are left:
- **H1:** the top's perturbation barely changes behaviour. Intent masks, holds and the bottom
  could absorb it.
- **H2:** behaviour changes, but a 10-minute energy score can't see it. Meals are rare events;
  the score is mostly "did prey pass".

They point to different fixes (H1: the brain's wiring or the stage; H2: the scoring window or
matched comparison). The design (`design/behaviour-ai-2026-10-11.md` §10.3, §11.7) says matched
copies are deterministic (`kin::matched::null_pair`: 32/32 staged pairs and 8/8 small live
windows bit-identical). Matched live windows are "the same machinery on a bigger scene".
LJ-SIGNAL found the live loop refuses `matched`; `SavedScene`, `matched_generation` and
`ascend_pairs` exist, but nothing loops them over live windows.

## Do

1. **Meal statistics** from the probe's `generations.jsonl` (`score.intake_normalized`,
   `delta_normalized`): the share of lanternjaw body-windows with any intake, and how much of
   the score variance the meal/no-meal split explains.
2. **A twin probe** (a measurement tool, not a training mode). From saved live starts (found
   with `--top-up lanternjaw=8:0.3`), run matched copies of one 10-minute window, the same
   start in each:
   - **all-sign:** every lanternjaw on `+ε_p` in one copy and `−ε_p` in the other;
   - **focal:** one lanternjaw carries `±ε_p`; every other body runs the centre.

   At σ 0.02 / 0.05 / 0.1, with several pairs p and several starts, report:
   - **behaviour:** the per-body intent shares and the number of intent switches in each twin,
     and the step at which each pair of twins first diverges;
   - **outcome:** the score difference per body (focal, and the others);
   - **consistency:** does the same `ε_p` help on different starts? Report an ICC over starts,
     the quantity per_body training needs.
   - Reuse the `kin::matched` machinery wherever it fits.
   - Keep each run on the quick lane; if the full set can't fit, submit it as one job with a
     DONE marker and hand back.
3. **Read H1 vs H2** from 1 and 2, with the numbers.
4. **Price the paths** (desktop thread-hours to reach a stated gradient quality, from your
   measured effect sizes):
   - training on matched live windows (what new code the trainer needs);
   - longer windows;
   - the staged scenes alone.

## Boundaries

- Measurement only. No change to the trainer's default behaviour, the menus, labels, reward
  terms or heuristics. Plain training stays byte-identical
  (`a_plain_lineage_trains_exactly_as_today`).
- New probe code is fine (an example or a switch that defaults off).
- A reward-term change is Wrysk's call: propose, don't build.
- EC2 is never yours.

## Return (at most 40 lines)

- The meal statistics, with how each was computed.
- The twin-probe table (σ × all-sign/focal: divergence step, intent-share differences, score
  differences, consistency ICC), with job ids and output paths.
- H1 or H2, and why.
- The price of each path, and your recommendation.
- Commits, tests, and every departure from this brief, with why.
