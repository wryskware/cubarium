# Voxel-era backlog: questions carried out of the flat world

The 2026-09-13 flat-era briefs `01`–`06` were deleted on 2026-09-18 with the
dated material. Their flat-world instantiations are dead; the questions survive
and are collected here so they are not lost. This is a backlog of questions, not
an accepted plan and not a set of tasks — pick **one** and brief it before
working. Adapt each question to the voxel world (`crates/cubarium-voxel*`); do
not port the old crates, cohorts or raw outputs, and do not resume old reduction
commands.

## 1. Ordinary ecosystem health

Does an unattended voxel world replace its ordinary plants and animals, and is
visible variety real rather than a presentation threshold? Diagnose with a small
read-only census (per-species population and plant stages) first, then one causal
question. Keep per-kind recruitment and plant-health measures; never equate high
total population with diversity or health. No quota populations, free offspring,
routine reseeding or stronger care dependence to hide decline.

## 2. Affordable, genuinely observable quiet

Does genuine rest occur at all, and at what frequency, tied to real intake and
reserve rather than a timer? Any candidate must distinguish living time, actual
rest, intake, births and recruitment. Restrained and local; no global wake-up
effect and no mandatory maintenance. The old birth-only candidate was too sparse
(~0.03 % of living time) and its flat-world shadow instrumentation is history.

## 3. A viable apex/rare lineage

Can a rare predator lineage recruit **paid** offspring to adulthood without
subsidies, protected prey or automatic replacement? Rarity around one or two is
the desired feel, not a licence for a population quota. Acceptance must show paid
descendants reaching adulthood, honest prey impact, and scarcity emerging from
resources. Coordinate with question 1: fragile ordinary prey are not a sound
predator baseline.

## 4. Readability of flexible plant motion

One clearly readable improvement at native size, preserving the established look:
one concrete pop, clip or unreadable bend, at most one candidate, accept/reject
recorded. "Smooth everything" and blanket coverage-AA are not targets. Check the
loop boundary, reversal and seam placement; a browser check does not prove panel
readability.

## 5. Readable care / rain response

One missing response to real water input that reads at native size, distinct from
a biological response that needs actual water/feeding conditions. Restrained,
local, interpolated, restart-continuous. No fake satiation, unconditional swarm,
global wake-up, attention punishment, or a toxin pool renamed from cleanup. Feed,
rain and litter cleanup already act on the shared world with accounting and
durable replay.

## 6. Autonomy / ambient support

How much does the world supply itself, independently of manual input size,
without making an attractive unattended world depend on chores? Document the
current parameter contract first, then one narrow hypothesis with a predeclared
seed/arm/horizon set and honest per-kind measures. Preserve the unchanged default
unless new evidence supports a change; do not ship a slider labelled "balanced"
on accounting tests alone or bury extra extinctions in pooled totals.

## Carried creative directions

From the old animation roadmap, kept as ideas for later, not obligations:
persistent plant life history (sprout, height, flowering, wilt, paid regrowth —
distinct from presentation growth clips); silhouette-scale fruiting and
seed-release; restrained local quiet rituals once their biological triggers
exist; selective thin-stalk AA only if a native-size comparison shows a gain; and
richer high-resolution or LCD presentation as exploration. Deferred product and
knob items live in [backlog.md](../backlog.md).
