# Deep terrariums: a natural lake outline, and the hall that went missing

Wrysk, 2026-10-07, on the 72- and 96-deep terrariums (merged f9f9c132, brief
`deep-world-2026-10-07.md`): "both d72 and d96 versions look excellent". Two problems:
"what happened to the cave on the deeper maps?" and "whats with the lake starting to look
real angular here?" He said go for both fixes.

Read `design/handoffs/worker-rules-2026-10-07.md` first.

## What we found

- **Lake**: `lake_bed` (`crates/cubarium-voxel/src/terrarium.rs`, around line 1297) is a
  rectangle in plan. Every column of a fixed x span runs from the glass (z = 0) back to the
  foot. Its sides are straight lines front to back. At depth 48 they were short and hidden.
  The lowland stretches them in plain view.
- **Cave**: the dark opening at the massif's foot in the 48-deep picture is a hall under a
  shelf, opening at the glass. `layout` (around line 1074) now puts the shelf's front at
  `added + share × massif depth`, so the hall's mouth faces the lowland from behind it. On
  the deeper maps it doesn't show. The likely cause is the lowland's blend ramp
  (`lowland_blend_m`) or the `before` height sampled at `zf` filling the mouth. Verify
  before fixing.

## Build

1. **A natural lake outline, at every depth.** The shore wanders: a smooth noise-warped
   outline whose width varies front to back. Through the lowland it may widen into a bay or
   narrow toward the glass; your call, state it. Bed depth comes from distance to the
   shore, so there are no straight banks or vertical walls in plan. The lake is still fed
   by the existing spring and river router, in the same closed cycle. Volume similar or a
   little larger. Every new shape number is a preset field (serde, doc comment with unit).
2. **The hall shows on the deep maps.** Its mouth opens onto the lowland unobstructed, at
   the same place around the ring as on the 48-deep map with the same seed. The camera
   (30°) must see into it: terrain never occludes terrain.
3. **Pictures** with the `deep_world` example: founding captures of seed 1 at depths 48, 72
   and 96, side by side, at the same size as
   `.claude/worktrees/deep-world/runs/deep-world-2026-10-07/side-by-side.png`. Also crops of
   the lake and the hall, before and after.

Don't touch the deep-world worktree: its cost batch is still running there.
