## Why

PG-PILOT (main 728c3bdc, `crates/cubarium-search/src/es/voxel/pg/`) ran 3 h on the lanternjaw
from its clone centre: `runs/lab/1014-023624-pg-pilot-lj-56fc/run/run` (read with
`python3 -I scripts/studies/pg_pilot_read.py --pg <dir>/run/run --es <es>/run/run --every 40`).
- 440 generations. Split cosine over bodies was +.018 against a null of −.014. The paired gain
  over the rules ranged −.009 to +.031 with no trend.
- Entropy rose from 0.11 to 1.62 (≈ ln 5) and 76% of samples were off the argmax: the policy
  went uniform. Normalised advantages are mostly noise, and Adam turns the small, consistent
  entropy gradient into full-size steps.
- The per-decision reward (own energy change plus kin) is almost flat from one decision to the
  next.

Wrysk's calls (`design/behaviour-ai-2026-10-11.md`, the "Score topic 1/2/3" bullets near
§0 end; research `design/7_Research/game-ai-research-2026-10-14.md` §6):
1. **Split the score** ("yeah split score"). The **judge** is outcomes only (condition,
   survival, offspring, kin); every comparison and every brain-vs-rules check uses it. The
   **teacher** is the per-decision reward: the judge per step plus extras that must raise the
   judge.
2. **Death costs a small life value** (taken on my recommendation). It is per lineage, in
   body_ref units, larger for breeding adults and the young, on top of the stored energy. The
   learned value baseline sits underneath. It belongs to the judge.
3. **Drives in potential form** ("yah lets do B and get baseline models … temperment weights
   later"). The teacher adds hunger (gut fill), wound load and sleep debt as drive before −
   drive after: one weight each, the same for every species. No per-species weights.
4. Parent's call, from research topic 6(c): **replace the entropy bonus with a pull toward the
   clone**, a KL from the clone's intent distribution on the same input. Its weight is a knob
   with an optional fade over generations (0 = constant). The default entropy for this run is
   0; keep the knob.

## Do

1. **Judge.** It is a reward computation over a window built from `RewardTerm`s
   (`pg/reward.rs`): own condition (today's energy term), the life value lost at a death, the
   life value of an own young born, and kin shares of all of these. The life value is per
   lineage and per stage (young, adult, breeding adult). Find in the code what "breeding"
   means: pregnant, or of breeding age. Every number is a serde knob in the training config
   with a doc comment (training side, like `pg/knobs.rs`, not FaunaConfig). Choose small
   defaults, and justify them in the commit message against body_ref and a typical window's
   energy change.
2. **Teacher.** It is the judge per step plus the three drive terms in potential form. Find
   where each drive lives in the fauna state: gut fill against capacity, wound load, and
   sleep debt (rest physiology, topic 5). Cite file:line. Read them as bookkeeping for the
   reward only; behaviour never reads them.
3. **KL pull to the clone** in `pg/learner.rs`, beside or replacing the entropy term. Keep
   its gradient tested by finite differences, as the entropy's is.
4. **Checks report the judge.** The brain-vs-rules check (gain/body) and `pg_pilot_read.py`
   show the judge per body, beside today's energy gain, for both brain and rules.
5. **Tests first, mechanism only**, no tuned numbers:
   - a death costs the life value and a birth adds the young's;
   - a drive term over a window telescopes to the drive's start − end;
   - the KL gradient matches finite differences, is zero at the clone, and pulls toward it;
   - the plain path and ES are unchanged (`a_plain_lineage_trains_exactly_as_today` passes);
   - pilot v1's config still reproduces its own reward (new terms off by default, or v1's
     config pins them off).
6. **Run v2:** the lanternjaw from the same clone centre, the same 24 check seeds, 3 h, with
   the new teacher and the KL pull. Use the inputs in `runs/pg-pilot-2026-10-14/inputs/` as
   the template, put new inputs in `runs/score-v2-2026-10-15/inputs/`, and submit on your
   branch tip. Hand back the job id.
   - Pass reading: split cosine clearly above its null; entropy stays near the clone's (no
     drift to uniform); a judge gain over the rules that rises over generations; no
     sit-still (the `mov` column); a diet that doesn't swing onto one prey.
