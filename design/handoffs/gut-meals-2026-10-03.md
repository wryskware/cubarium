# Build the minimal gut and realistic metabolism

Wrysk chose this on 2026-10-03: "go with the gut version".

**The design is `design/metabolism-meals-2026-10-02.md`.** Read all of it. Its "Repair 2:
the implementation spec" section is binding and overrides anything above it that
contradicts it.

Also read Astra's review `design/7_Research/astra-metabolism-meals-review-2026-10-02.md`,
including the Repair 1 check. Astra reviews **your implementation** afterwards, so build to
that section.

## Scope

**In:**
- **The stomach:** per-food-class pools of raw organic matter, mineral and energy, separate
  from the existing egesta `gut`.
- **Digestion** into reserve room only, with the cap formula.
- **Appetite** (`s_start` from `s0` and reserve hunger) and the **eating latch**.
- **The precedence:** safety and alert, the emergency latch, off-phase sleep, on-phase
  wanting or not. Hunting, luring, pack target acquisition and flyer sipping all go through
  the gate. The hunters' satiated rest goes (`rest_at`, `wake_at`, and `torpor_upkeep`
  between meals).
- **Pack hunt start:** at least half the adults wanting, or any adult in emergency.
- **Regurgitation** of stomach food to pup stomachs, with the reserve transfer as fallback.
- **Riders:** `parasite_take_per_day` 0.09 of host body per world day, into the rider's
  reserve.
- **New registry rows,** per lineage unless marked:
  - `daily_need` `f`;
  - stomach `k`;
  - `τ` per food class;
  - bite `b`, per current body;
  - `s0`;
  - `full_tolerance` 0.98;
  - `emergency_clear_hunger` 0.6;
  - `parasite_take_per_day`, global.
  - `maintenance_per_s` derives from `f / day_length_s`. If keeping it as the stored row is
    simpler, keep it, and set it to `f / 7200`.
- **Defaults** from the design's tables: `f`, `k`, `τ`, `b = 2k/τ`, and the lifespans in
  repair 2 item 5.
- **Live presets:** in the four presets' curiosity-gate blocks
  (`config/desktop/terrarium.toml`, `config/desktop/voxel.toml`,
  `config/voidrunner/terrarium.toml`, `config/tachyon/voxel.toml`), **remove** the
  per-lineage keys the new model supersedes:
  - `maintenance_per_s`;
  - `motor_respiration_per_s`;
  - `bite_per_s`;
  - `lifespan_median_s`.

  The code defaults then apply. Keep the block's other leaves (plants, decomposition,
  yields, reproduction, growth) and add a comment saying why.
- **Books** for organic matter, mineral and energy, including stomach, transfers and
  respiration.
- **Snapshot serialisation** of stomach and latches, with a fauna schema bump.
- **Readouts** in `founder_tally.rs`, per lineage:
  - meals per world day;
  - bout kinds;
  - stomach fullness over the day;
  - emergency wakes;
  - sleep flips per hour;
  - maturation time.
- Regenerate `design/parameters.md`.

**Out:**
- `crates/cubarium-search` and `config/training/search/*`. The rebase is a second package.
- The sense contract (`design/voxel-senses.md`): brains observe hunger as today.
- Food-quality flavour beyond `τ` and yield.

## Tests (write first, commit failing, then implement; don't weaken them)

Use repair 2 item 12, all of it, in controlled fixtures: fixed adult body, breeding off,
lifespan off.
- **Interval:** at least 3 completed meal intervals per lineage. The mean must be within
  ±30 % of Astra's exact intervals:

  | lineage | interval (world days) |
  |---|---|
  | bellwing | 0.1012 |
  | seedporter | 0.3162 |
  | frondgrazer | 0.5060 |
  | loftstrider | 0.6324 |
  | littershredder | 0.5407 |
  | capgnawer | 0.5407 |
  | ripple-snail | 0.4866 |
  | chorister | 1.2649 |
  | lanternjaw | 3.0415 |

- **The rest of item 12:**
  - a full stomach on food takes no bite;
  - a fed sleeper sleeps through its phase;
  - a starving sleeper wakes, refills and makes at most 2 sleep flips an hour;
  - a sated hunter starts no lure, target or strike;
  - no prey means no strike;
  - interrupted access keeps the latch on;
  - famine recovery: `0 ≤ S ≤ K`, and the books close;
  - regurgitation conserves and keeps composition;
  - a rider on a fixed 1.5-ou host reaches drop-off in about 1.19 world days, ±30 %;
  - a juvenile's maturation time is measured;
  - the books close over one world day for organic matter, mineral and energy.
- **Existing tests** that pin the old satiated-rest thresholds, hunger-scaled bites or old
  lifespans: change them only where the design changes the behaviour, and say which ones.
