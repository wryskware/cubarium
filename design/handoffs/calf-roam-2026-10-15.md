# CALF-ROAM: older calves wander farther and more often, bracketed to a rare feast

## Rules

Read `design/handoffs/behaviour-ai-worker-rules-2026-10-12.md` first (the 2026-10-12 rules hold).
- Make the worktree from main (`git worktree add .claude/worktrees/calf-roam -b calf-roam main`),
  then seed its `target/` with `cp -a --reflink=auto` from the main checkout's. Use -j 8 or lower.
- Never wrap `scripts/lab/lab` in `timeout`. Quick lane only for what you run yourself
  (`quick = true`, `hosts = ["desktop"]`, `cwd` set, under 15 minutes). Batches are
  submitted, then you hand back. Never wait on them. No tool call over 5 minutes.
- A `command` job whose argv is a local path must pin `hosts = ["desktop"]` (on eidolon it
  exits 127). `batch` jobs with `binary = { tree, commit }` can use both hosts.
- Never write "chorister" except where code needs the identifier. The animal is the Veilhound.

## Why

Topic 7 and topic 8 (`design/behaviour-ai-2026-10-11.md` §0 rows 7–8, §3.5, and the
"Calves and family movement" bullet near §0 end). Wrysk, 2026-10-14: calves never strayed past
their excursion distance in 48 world-hours. "B for now works i guess": older calves wander
farther and more often, bracketed to about **one loftstrider per world-year per world, mostly
strayed calves**. Topic 8 also wants a standoff every 1–2 h that usually ends with the pack
giving up, and a hound kicked dead about once in a few years. These are targets to bracket,
not gates (memory "targets are to reach": sweeps extend until they bracket).
- Families now move. LS-BROWSE (main c507ad00, ecology thread) means loftstriders pick leaves
  slowly and leave stripped stands; they walked 3.8× farther in a 3 h A/B. The calf work
  waited on this.
- Fauna schema is 89. The old calf-cont m183 saves no longer load.
