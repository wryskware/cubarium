## 1. Correct the actual movement contract

`motor.rs::MotorLimits::capability` currently adds
`REFERENCE_RADIUS_PX * turn_rate_max` to `speed_cap`. Remove the independent
rotation allowance. Available capability is the translational capability after
effort, morphology, environment and legitimate bursts, capped by affordable energy.
Resolve requests so `|v| + r*|omega| <= available capability`. Retain angular limits
as additional ceilings, never an addition to capability. Pure pivot remains valid.

Preserve physical body extent, paid actual motion, heat accounting and unpaid
seam transport. Check every final override path through the common resolver.
Zero requests produce stillness; nonzero legacy resting effort is not literally
zero. A Mode label must neither supply free rotation nor suppress legitimate escape.
Do not add acceleration state, a contact solver or a neural ABI in this slice.

Keep existing speed, upkeep, movement price and rotation-price coefficient (0.5)
for this measurement. Do not restore fast rotation to preserve legacy behavior.
If corrected turning invalidates a hunter timing fixture, adjust its setup/timeout
to exercise physically feasible capture and explain the change. Keep valid contact,
funding and windup assertions; do not disable the suite. A runtime timeout change
must be a narrow justified integration correction, not a new hunting heuristic.
Report degraded legacy pursuit and any need to reconsider pace separately.

Send Fable a short factual note when the motor semantics are fixed: capability,
supported directions, requests versus resolved motion, radius and relevant units.

## 2. Measure whether physical travel can pay

Add one narrowly scoped diagnostic beside `examples/food_stock_flow.rs`, reusing
the actual core. Use a matched finite 3x3 patch with documented initial stocks,
light, water, nutrients, body, stores, diet and fixture interventions. A scripted
grazer travels between neighboring cells and leaves below a fixed disclosed
threshold. Scripts are diagnostic probes, not new live behavior. Travel and turning
must pass through the corrected resolver; no teleporting or free alignment.

At most four arms, each at most 36,000 ticks (1,800 simulated seconds):

1. Stationary grazer with the existing legacy feeding gate, as a reference.
2. Stationary continuous grazing request without that behavioral gate.
3. Mobile scripted grazing with the same continuous request semantics.
4. Matched no-intake control to measure reserve-funded survival.

All arms retain real physical/physiological limits and existing world-side intake
response. If no diagnostic intent override exists, add only the small transient
seam needed to exercise these requests; keep it out of persisted state and ordinary
live policy. Do not remove a world capacity/funding constraint to bypass a behavioral
gate. Match motion in the no-intake arm to the mobile script where feasible; disclose
the schedule and why it gives the appropriate survival baseline. Disable births
equally in these individual-capability fixtures; account for all initial resources.

Leave producer/regrowth rules, cell resolution, handling prices and cropping floors
unchanged. Measure whether continuous requests actually create the suspected issue.
Recovery of abandoned cells can be observed within the mobile arm; report censoring
if revisits or the horizon prevent a clean recovery estimate. No extra recovery arms.

Report actual per-food intake, local stocks, gross renewal if available, usable-store
changes, upkeep, movement/rotation expenditure, cell transitions, physical turns,
cycle yield, survival and recovery. Use consistent material/energy/time units. Do
not infer intake from Feeding mode or reserve changes alone. Distinguish behavior,
mouth throughput, resource quality, environmental limits and starting stores.

Cap diagnostic execution at two workers and 120 seconds total wall time, including
retries; compilation and relevant correctness tests are separate. Poll cancellation
within runs. Failure, starvation or an unprofitable cycle is a valid measured result.
Do not adjust the fixture until it succeeds, multiply seeds or rerun the six-run
population experiment. Stop at the cap with incomplete quantities identified.
