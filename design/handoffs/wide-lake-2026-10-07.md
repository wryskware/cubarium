# More lake in the wide terrarium

Wrysk, 2026-10-07, on the 640-wide terrarium (`config/desktop/terrarium-wide.toml`, merged
cd481545): "in the wide world, id maybe raise the water table? just that proportionally, i
think theres more room for lake. even future aquatic animals. otherwise it looks amazing".

Read `design/handoffs/worker-rules-2026-10-07.md` first.

## Build

Change the wide config only (overrides under `[world.landform]`). The 256-wide worlds stay as
they are.

1. **A proportionally bigger lake.** Raise `water_level_m` (the water table: the lake drains
   into the aquifer through its floor, and everything open below the line floods). Enlarge
   the outline to suit: `lake_m`, `lake_width_share`, `lake_bay`, `lake_depth_m` and the
   other lake fields in `crates/cubarium-voxel/src/terrarium.rs`. Aim for a real body of
   water in the lowland, with shallows and a deeper middle, and room for aquatic animals
   later. The rocks and the lowland hills should still read as land.
   - Groves need their roots above the table. Report the generator's grove sites before
     and after.
   - The closed water cycle stays closed. Check that founding fills the lake and that it
     holds at 30 sim-minutes, not draining away or flooding the plain.
2. **Keep the lake whole on screen.** With seed 1 the lake straddles the ring's seam, which
   is the screen's left and right edge, so on the flat 2560 screen it shows as two half
   ponds. Add a preset knob that keeps the lake's whole reach clear of the seam. Default
   off, so current worlds don't change; on in the wide config. The lake stays off-centre
   (Wrysk rejected centring it before).
3. **Pictures** at 2560×720 (the `deep_world` example, `raster=2560x720`): two or three
   water levels stacked in one image at founding, then the chosen one at founding and at
   30 minutes, for seeds 1 and 2. State the values you chose.

The lake-cave worktree has a cost batch running on slot 0. Don't rebuild there; use slot 1
for your runs.

## Return

Commits, the chosen values, grove sites before and after, picture paths.
