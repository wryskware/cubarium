# A 640-wide, 96-deep terrarium for the desktop's 2560×720 screen

Wrysk, 2026-10-07: "i want to consider a world that has an aspect ratio to go on a 2560x720
screen, either d72 or d96. my lean is still d96 if it runs okay". Of the two options offered,
he chose A: **640 wide × 128 tall × 96 deep at 0.125 m, drawn at 4 px per voxel**. At 4 px
the depth step is 2 px, so the strip is 2560 × (128·4 + 96·2) = 2560 × 704 and fills the
screen's width. "this would be desktop. voidrunner remains 1920x1200."

Read `design/handoffs/worker-rules-2026-10-07.md` first. Do this after the lake and hall fix,
on top of it, as separate commits.

## Build

1. **Config**: `config/desktop/terrarium-wide.toml`, identical to
   `config/desktop/terrarium-d96.toml` except width 640 and `px_per_voxel = 4` (auto would
   size for the largest connected screen, the 4K one). No voidrunner twin.
2. **The ring at 80 m instead of 32 m.** The terrarium's ring features are counted per world
   (spires, shelves, arches, lake length, the spring and its course). At 2.5× the
   circumference a fixed count leaves the ring sparse. Make them scale with circumference,
   so 256-wide worlds come out as they do now. One lake system or two on an 80 m ring:
   your call; state it and show it. Metre wavelengths stay as they are.
3. **Founders**: check whether the founding recipe (plants, animals, packs) scales with
   area or uses fixed counts. If fixed, scale it with area the same way, so 256-wide worlds
   are unchanged. This is not ecology tuning; say what you changed.
4. **Pictures**: a headless founding capture at 2560×720 (the strip at 4 px), and one at
   about 30 sim-minutes.
5. **Cost**: the same measurement as the deep-world batch (`scripts/deep-world-batch.py`):
   step ms/tick with its split at minutes 0–5 and 55–60, 16 threads on one slot, plus render
   and pack at 2560×720. A detached batch that writes its table, then `DONE`. Check `uptime`
   first; another batch may still be running on slot 1.
