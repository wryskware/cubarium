# 06 — Optional autonomy tuning

Outcome: optionally change how much the ecosystem supplies itself, independently
of manual input size, without making the attractive unattended world dependent on
chores. Manual doses already exist. No tuned hands-on autonomy preset is shipped.

The reviewed two-hour ambient-support comparison found two additional no-care
skimmer losses at 90% support (seeds 5 and 10). That is an adverse result, not a
recommendation to lower the default. The 24-hour processes finished, but biological
interpretation was not completed before their raw outputs were deleted. There is
no established 24-hour conclusion. Live ambient support remains unchanged.

## First bounded milestone

Start after task 01 has clarified unattended fragility, or limit this slice to
documenting the current parameter contract. Inspect how ambient support and manual
doses enter accounting, and define one narrow hypothesis about the proposed tuning.
Reuse compact two-hour findings; do not reproduce the old cohort as a prerequisite.

If a new comparison is justified, predeclare a small seed/arm/horizon set, the
per-kind recruitment and plant-health measures, and a stop condition. Use one normal
build cache and compact output. Any expansion to long cohorts is a separate decision,
not an automatic next step. Do not ship a slider labelled “balanced” on accounting
tests alone or bury extra extinctions in pooled population totals.
