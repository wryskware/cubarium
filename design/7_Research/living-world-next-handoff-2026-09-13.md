### Astra early geometry finding

Independently traveled part anchors do not automatically share vertex ownership:
`unfold_pixels` chooses the shortest chart per anchor. Prefer one root-owned
surface-pixel query for the whole rig; evaluate articulated parts in that shared
body chart and composite in explicit depth order. Each part's filtered local
support remains ≤9 px, while the aggregate query can use the surface library's
actual 32 px limit. Reflect the moving root at the open bottom rim, not static
limb offsets; artwork clips there. Respect the documented localized top-vertex
chart cut rather than promising curvature-free rigid wrapping. Full Astra review
will specify bounds/tests.
