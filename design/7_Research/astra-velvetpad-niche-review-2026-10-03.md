Review of `design/velvetpad-niche-2026-10-03.md`. Evidence below is from **branch `plants3`, head c0454fce**, read without checking it out; the diagnostic was also checked at **ade04225**. This is review evidence, not an accepted design decision. Static code and arithmetic review only: I have not rerun the reported world or independently verified its counts.

The wet-ground direction is coherent. The proposed numbers do not yet establish a persistent shaded niche, and restricting establishment does not ensure replacement of existing mats. A crash is a risk, not a demonstrated outcome: sufficiently wet, moderately shaded sites can fund velvetpad, while much of the quoted 16% need not qualify.

1. **Important — Q1: distinguish the measured configuration from default-species arithmetic.** Evidence: `plants3:crates/cubarium/examples/stand_niche_diag.rs:21–27` loads the saved flora configuration. `plants3:crates/cubarium-voxel-flora/src/lib.rs:1900–1909` gives the default, full-foliage reference calculation: at nutrient Monod factor 2/3, zero aeration stress, unit daylight and temperature multiplier, maintenance plus replacement of senesced foliage requires `L_eff × μ ≥ 0.2625`, rising to `0.4875` at senescence 0.001. That calculation is correct under those assumptions, but is not a daily survival threshold. The desktop preset overrides velvetpad's alpha, assimilation, maintenance and senescence to 4, 0.00655842, 0.000327921 and 0.000580779 (`plants3:config/desktop/terrarium.toml:248–259`); the corresponding reference threshold is **0.1781**, or **0.2932** after setting senescence to 0.001. Bloomcrown's preset senescence is 0.00179320 (`:134–145`), so 0.001 is not the common live value. “Shade costs it nothing” is also incorrect: `L_eff = L(1+h)/(L+h)` makes raw light 0.05 yield only 0.3667 at h=0.1 (`plants3:crates/cubarium-voxel-flora/src/step.rs:989–997`). **Change:** publish the diagnostic snapshot's resolved configuration, label the reference assumptions, and specify whether the patch changes defaults, the terrarium overrides, or both. Correct the diagnostic path to `crates/cubarium/examples/stand_niche_diag.rs`.

2. **Blocking — Q1/Q3: the proposed establishment threshold admits energetically poor sites; water units and the aeration change need precision.** Evidence: available water is normalized between wilting point and **field capacity**, with 1 meaning field capacity, not pore saturation (`plants3:crates/cubarium-voxel-flora/src/step.rs:1710–1718`). With wilt/full 0.9/1.3, establishment at water 1.0 gives `μ = 0.25` (`:1799–1805`). At the proposed upper light boundary 0.6, `L_eff = 0.9429`, giving `L_eff × μ = 0.2357`: below both post-change reference thresholds in finding 1. Using the default threshold, water must reach approximately **1.107 at raw light 0.6**, or **1.255 at raw light 0.1**; raw light 0.05 cannot meet that full-foliage reference budget even at full moisture. With the desktop overrides these water requirements are approximately **1.024, 1.113 and 1.220**, respectively. These are optimistic constant-day, nongrowing reference budgets, not guaranteed establishment values; actual income also depends on minerals, canopy, day/night and reserve-funded growth (`:2591–2629`). **Change:** measure viable establishment and donor recruitment across water/light cells with the actual resolved preset before selecting the thresholds; align the establishment gate with that viable band or explicitly accept and quantify seedling sinks. Rename “near saturation” to the correct water units. Use the actual knob `establish_saturated_max = 1.0`: it already makes the aeration target zero (`:1779–1784`). Retain relaxation so existing stress can clear; do not implement “off” by freezing both rates. Keep the distinct 0.1 m drowning limit explicit (`plants3:crates/cubarium-voxel-flora/src/lib.rs:1928–1929`).

3. **Blocking — Q2: an establishment ceiling selects birth sites, not adult sun intolerance or competitive displacement.** Evidence: the current establishment light gate uses **terrain sky visibility**, not canopy-transmitted light (`plants3:crates/cubarium-voxel-flora/src/step.rs:3340–3376`). Adult growth uses canopy-attenuated light and a monotonically increasing response (`:680–708`, `:989–997`). Adult dieback comes from unpaid maintenance, not failing establishment gates (`:2827–2846`). Thus a ceiling of 0.6 would exclude even a shaded grove beneath trees on terrain with open sky, while an established pad in a bright, wet hollow could remain well funded. Existing light competition cannot make extra sunlight intrinsically harmful. **Change:** define whether the intended shade is terrain shelter or tree canopy. I recommend testing the water-budget niche first, retaining the existing shade-tolerant response; add a terrain ceiling only if terrain shelter is explicitly the intended birth-site restriction. If the requirement includes retreat of existing mats from sunny wet sites, specify and test an adult mechanism separately, rather than claiming the ceiling achieves it. Any canopy-based gate must use structural/transmitted light, not instantaneous nighttime darkness. Apply a new birth gate consistently to seedlings and runners; spore landing deliberately ignores the light gate (`plants3:crates/cubarium-voxel-flora/src/seeds.rs:993–1012`), so landing and establishment must remain distinguished.

4. **Important — Q1/Q2: occupied-site priority is still a substantial advantage, and “every other plant outcompetes it” is not implemented.** Evidence: seedlings can enter an occupied ground-cover site only when their species has positive `overtop_per_check`; the weighted lottery winner then gets the overtopping draw, followed by the configured arming process (`plants3:crates/cubarium-voxel-flora/src/seeds.rs:399–444`). The default overtopping probability is zero (`plants3:crates/cubarium-voxel-flora/src/lib.rs:1461–1462`); springturf, stonecushion and umbrellafrond do not acquire a positive override. Runners also require vacant sites (`plants3:crates/cubarium-voxel-flora/src/seeds.rs:803–847`). Senescence removes foliage into litter, not the occupied woody body (`plants3:crates/cubarium-voxel-flora/src/step.rs:2805–2846`). Velvetpad also keeps its 30% foliage-capacity grazing refuge and rapid growth/propagule settings (`plants3:crates/cubarium-voxel-flora/src/lib.rs:1946–1956`). **Change:** test both colonization of bare sites and invasion of established velvetpad by each named competitor. If retreat under water stress creates enough vacancies, show that trajectory; otherwise propose an explicit replacement mechanism for nonwoody competitors. Do not assume the woody 0.1 draw applies to every seed or guarantees replacement. Correct “30% clonal growth” to a 30% attempt to deliver a funded package clonally, with fallback dispersal (`plants3:crates/cubarium-voxel-flora/src/seeds.rs:664–710`). Avoid simultaneously reducing all growth, refuge and dispersal knobs before isolating which advantage remains.

5. **Important — Q3: 16% is neither a viable-area estimate nor drought insurance.** Evidence: the diagnostic bins all soil support faces using velvetpad's root box, labels shade by terrain sky <0.6, and gives the saturated-fraction bin precedence over mean water (`plants3:crates/cubarium/examples/stand_niche_diag.rs:36–59`). Its shaded wet/soggy count therefore does not test the proposed joint gates, minimum light, drowning limit, productive light/water combination, or seed access. Spores sample column supports and runners need reachable vacant neighbors (`plants3:crates/cubarium-voxel-flora/src/seeds.rs:803–847`, `:1019–1054`); not every counted support is equally reachable. Water is a current state, not a permanent land classification. Default seeds expire after 10,800 s, with continuous attrition; the desktop override shortens this to 6,587 s (`plants3:crates/cubarium-voxel-flora/src/lib.rs:1944–1945`; `plants3:config/desktop/terrarium.toml:256–257`). **Change:** report the actual viable, reachable intersection and its persistence over time, including donor recruitment and seed-bank replacement. Include a dry spell and recovery spanning seed-bank expiry. Require surviving reproductive refuges or demonstrated recolonization; do not promise drought robustness from an instantaneous area count.

6. **Important — Q4: the food-security question is unanswered, and the area reduction is misstated.** If all 2,084 sites became occupied, that is only **27.1% of the reported 7,687 occupied sites**, a 72.9% reduction, not halving. It is not itself a production estimate. Evidence: velvetpad has a 0.125 m crown and 30% grazing refuge (`plants3:crates/cubarium-voxel-flora/src/lib.rs:1953–1956`); replacing its area with taller foliage does not necessarily replace accessible intake. The historical “58% of browser intake” comment (`:1957–1959`) is not a current diet measurement. The balance implementation measures actual assimilation relative to costs and reproductive funding, not plant area (`plants3:crates/cubarium-search/src/es/voxel/live/balance.rs:137–180`). **Change:** run paired fauna worlds and report each consumer's intake by plant and layer, accessible edible production, B, R-hat, access, sated share, births and reserve/body trends. Compare matched pre-change worlds and the established post-change community. No numerical starvation clearance can be given from this plants-only snapshot. Also track litter supply: higher senescence initially increases shedding, while losing most pads can subsequently reduce it (`plants3:crates/cubarium-voxel-flora/src/step.rs:2805–2821`), affecting decomposers and their consumers.

7. **Important — Q5: occupancy at 6 h is a useful endpoint, but insufficient acceptance evidence.** Evidence: establishment, maintenance failure, overtopping and reserve-funded reproduction are distinct processes (`plants3:crates/cubarium-voxel-flora/src/step.rs:3343–3376`, `:2827–2846`; `plants3:crates/cubarium-voxel-flora/src/seeds.rs:399–444`, `:664–710`). A surviving founder or initially stored seed can conceal failure to reproduce. **Change:** use deterministic boundary fixtures for water/light/drowning, aeration relaxation, seed and runner establishment, and overtopping; then a small full-cycle physiology grid covering wet-shaded survival, dry-site decline and bright-wet adult behavior. For seeds 1/3/5/6, use paired baseline/candidate starts and time series through 6 h, not only terminal occupancy. Include both fresh founders and a saved invaded world, with an explicit, verified configuration replacement on load. Record births, donor counts, deaths by cause, reserve/foliage/wood, propagule funding, litter, and occupation transitions alongside raw terrain light, actual canopy light, mean water and saturation. First verify that the intended band sustains replacement and that non-niche sites actually change occupants, then run the paired fauna balance measure from finding 6. Specify acceptable retreat and persistence before evaluating the results; neither 16% occupancy nor survival alone should be the pass condition.
