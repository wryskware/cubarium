## Findings

### P1 — L falsifies the proposed score term, but not by establishing that the search cannot move

Both checks are useful, and their narrower results hold.

- Check (a) uses real observations, varies one food input family at a time, and evaluates reset and carried recurrent state through the core's own forward and squash functions (`ecology-v1-score-checks-2026-09-16.md:90-103`). The one-tick sampling lag is measured rather than ignored: the worst action reconstruction residual is \(1.84\times10^{-5}\), while the reported movement effects are 59 and 160 times larger (`ecology-v1-score-checks-2026-09-16.md:105-117`). Thus the small one-step sensitivities are real.
- Normalising \(\Delta\) by the held action's standard deviation is a reasonable descriptive comparison, but not the behavioral threshold. That standard deviation is itself produced by this nearly constant policy. The decisive scale for thrust is the 0.05 adapter deadband: moving local food from empty to full changes thrust by only 0.00109 around 0.52, nowhere near stopping the body (`ecology-v1-score-checks-2026-09-16.md:121-160`). For turn, the meaningful checks are whether the raw head crosses its deadband and whether heading changes enough to alter residence. The report gives those absolute comparisons, so its table is usable; \(\Delta/\mathrm{sd}\) should not carry the causal claim by itself.
- Check (b) is a valid falsifier of my proposed dense score term. On the four training layouts the existing score rises from 23,490 at dwell 20 to 36,000 at dwell 1,000; on all twelve it rises from 12,951 to 27,362, then falls at the over-long 3,000-tick dwell (`ecology-v1-score-checks-2026-09-16.md:196-247`). The pre-registered auxiliary spans only 200 ticks and preserves the ladder's ordering (`forager-score-proposal-2026-09-16.md:38-50,97-120`). It is unnecessary and was correctly marked “not proposed.”

The causal synthesis is stronger than those checks. The sweep is a one-forward-pass partial intervention at sampled states. It does not test sustained food input, recurrent integration across controller updates, a trajectory under the altered action, or whether nearby weight perturbations produce useful residence. L itself says the ladder consists entirely of competent route-followers and does not measure the score landscape near generation 9 (`ecology-v1-score-checks-2026-09-16.md:253-261,295-308`). “The controller cannot express that behaviour at all” and “the search is not converting [the behavioral gradient] into any movement in parameter space” therefore do not follow (`ecology-v1-score-checks-2026-09-16.md:263-278`). The consolidated result repeats the binding-constraint claim as established (`ecology-v1-round3-results-2026-09-16.md:320-336`).

More importantly, L's named next measurement is already stored. `GenerationReport` persists the 32 ordered `candidate_scores` and every candidate-layout episode (`crates/cubarium-search/src/es/trainer.rs:231-254,560-656`). I checked all 16 retained rows in `runs/es-eco-v1-fastleaf/generations.jsonl`:

| generation(s) checked | candidate-score standard deviation | min–max |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| 0–15 | 398–834 ticks | widest 6,400–10,276 |
| 9 | **643 ticks** | **6,459–8,915** |

For generation 9, candidate score correlates \(r=0.812\) with mean `intake_producer` across the four training layouts and \(r=0.544\) with mean `ticks_in_opening` (32 candidates, computed from the retained `jobs`). This is not proof that ES can discover residence—the score is the minimum across layouts while those correlates are means, and the old episode record has no on-food fraction—but it rules out “the perturbations produce no material score or feeding variation” as the remaining binary explanation. The zero-simulation task at `ecology-v1-score-checks-2026-09-16.md:316-321` should not be dispatched as new work.

The next analysis should use the existing antithetic pairs: relate each plus/minus score difference to intake, opening residence, deadband occupancy, and the signed contribution to the update; then re-evaluate only generation 9's 32 reconstructible candidates with H's residence trace if on-food time is required. Confirmation of an optimiser/update problem would be useful residence and score improvements in individual perturbations that cancel in the centred-rank gradient or are erased by the update. Refutation would be no candidate-level increase in on-food residence despite the score spread, which would move attention to parameterisation, recurrence, cadence, or the adapter.

### P2 — M proves what the crossings are, but option B is not yet an equilibrium rule

M's central conclusion follows even without the confounded absent arm. In the herbivore-present worlds, all 73 crossing cells have zero exact foliage withdrawal over the whole 180,000 ticks and a negative measured plant budget over the 6,000 ticks preceding the crossing; meanwhile animals withdraw 1,511–2,380 m per run from hundreds of other cells (`ecology-v1-plant-budget-2026-09-16.md:205-245`). That is sufficient to say these crossings were not caused by direct grazing. The absent arm changes nutrient inputs when it removes founders, as M correctly flags (`ecology-v1-plant-budget-2026-09-16.md:329-339`); it corroborates location and direction but is not needed for the causal exclusion.

Recording the terms in `fields.rs::react` is the right implementation boundary: it observes the actual §4 computation instead of duplicating it, while the four §6.4 hooks book the exact `q` at the stock-withdrawal sites (`crates/cubarium-core/src/fields.rs:209-322,416-432`; `crates/cubarium-core/src/world/step.rs:1863-1907,1961-2006`). The reported identities close to \(1.2\times10^{-11}\), and record-on, record-off, and I's old rows match by state hash (`ecology-v1-round3-results-2026-09-16.md:173-188`). I also reran the focused core and search plant-budget tests; all 21 passed. The instrument is adequately inert for this use.

The over-seeding diagnosis should be stated precisely: §11's uniform fraction gives some cells an opening foliage stock that their ensuing coupled field trajectory does not retain. M does not show a scalar, cell-autonomous equilibrium. It explicitly says tick 180,000 is not a fixed point (`ecology-v1-plant-budget-2026-09-16.md:340-342`), yet option B proposes fitting that endpoint to effective light, moisture, \(N\), and \(W\), then calls the result a local equilibrium (`ecology-v1-plant-budget-2026-09-16.md:390-430`). Those predictors are endogenous and spatially coupled: nutrients diffuse, propagules arrive, wood and reserve evolve, weather varies, and changing initial \(P\) changes the later state being fitted. A terminal regression may be a useful initializer, but “seeded at it stays there” is a new hypothesis, not a consequence of M.

I would put no universal critical \(L\mu\) in the contract. If §11 changes, define a deterministic **whole-field plant-only preconditioning operator**, not a per-cell critical constant: initialize the full §11 field, advance the ordinary §4 dynamics with animals absent for a declared seed/config-bound duration or until declared moving-window stock and budget criteria are met, then found animals from that joint \((P,W,Q,N,F,D,\ldots)\) state. If the field remains dynamic, call the result a fixed-age preconditioned opening, not equilibrium. The opening frame, founder census, total foliage, and first-hour plant-budget trajectory should be compared with status quo before acceptance.

The owner options otherwise state the visible tradeoff fairly: either alternative opens heterogeneous and roughly 3.6–4.2 times greener overall, rather than uniformly green and then fading (`ecology-v1-plant-budget-2026-09-16.md:364-414`). My advice to Wrysk is **choose neither as a contract change yet**. Test option A at a small set of fixed plant-only ages against status quo; do not adopt option B's endpoint fit as “local equilibrium.” If the preconditioned frame and founder outcomes are acceptable, A is the more faithful change because it preserves the coupled equations rather than replacing them with a fitted per-cell proxy. Until then, keep §11 and rename the counter's ungrazed branch as opening-stock decline; do not interpret it as herbivore depletion.

### P2 — N identifies the present stopper; the escape multiple is only provisionally exonerated

The record and classification are sound. Its 449 death-arm attempts reconcile exactly with K: 46 resolve in reach, 30 begin in reach and resolve out, 217 are classified `prey_outran`, 155 begin out of reach, and one loses its target (`ecology-v1-apex-reach-2026-09-16.md:150-183`). `prey_outran` is a geometric outcome—separation increased—not evidence that prey beat an active lunge; the table makes that visible because 213/217 such attempts were held.

The source supports the mechanism. The current `inside` test is the one-sided inequality `body.x < capture_offset_body.x + tolerance`; when true it selects resting effort and suppresses the strike boost (`crates/cubarium-core/src/world/step.rs:1170-1201`). It held on 408/449 death-arm paid attempts. During those held attempts median hunter speed was 0.002 px/s and the mean gap changed by −0.91 px in the wrong direction (`ecology-v1-apex-reach-2026-09-16.md:211-245`). The turn at the claw-derived radius can consume the whole rest-effort motor envelope, which closes the explanation (`ecology-v1-apex-reach-2026-09-16.md:247-297`). “The pursuit predicate suppresses the paid burst” follows.

The escape multiple is exonerated as the cause of the **current** near-zero motion, but not yet as adequate after the predicate is corrected. A nominal 16.667 px/s hunter against a 10 px/s prey has only 6.667 px/s relative closure; over the one-second strike it cannot close the reported mean 8.32 px starting gap. N itself computes 1.25 s at the escape cap, longer than `strike_seconds = 1.0`, and reports that the few delivered attempts are clipped to 5.7 px/s mean and 13.31 px/s maximum by the shared motor envelope (`ecology-v1-apex-reach-2026-09-16.md:299-312`). Therefore “escape speed changes nothing while the hunter is held” is correct; “the strike constants are adequate” is not established for the corrected arm.

The proposed paired arm—change only `inside` to `in_contact()` and retain N's kinematics plus K's ledger—is exactly the right experiment. Add relative closure during delivered bursts and capture opportunity by initial gap bin. I would tell Wrysk to approve the one-line correction if it materially reduces the held fraction, moves the gap toward zero, and produces captures without worsening lifetime/energy margin. It need not immediately achieve ten captures per life to show the predicate is wrong; break-even is the later ecological acceptance gate. Do not alter escape speed, sense radius, strike duration, or mating radius in that arm.

### P2 — O isolates a large depth effect, but not all of F or detrital viability

The 4×8 XOR assignment is a valid complete counterbalance for the two binary loci. Every cell receives all four treatments once, each row contains each treatment twice, and row pairs exchange one factor at a time (`crates/cubarium-search/src/factorial.rs:103-136`; `ecology-v1-depth-factorial-2026-09-16.md:120-139`). The four rows within one seed are divergent world trajectories, not independent replicates; O says so and correctly reports within-cell pairs plus agreement across four seed/world replicates. The focused factorial suite passed all 29 tests, including the Latin-square and one-locus-swap checks.

The retained treatment table supports a strong founding-time effect. At both diets, moving depth 0.10 to 0.55 changes median net margin from negative to positive in every world; the depth-0.55 treatment wins 30/32 founder-diet pairs and 29/32 foliage-diet pairs (`ecology-v1-depth-factorial-2026-09-16.md:240-275,304-337`). The interaction is also real in this design: at depth 0.10, diet 0.60 beats 0.85 on net margin in 25/32 cells, whereas at depth 0.55 diet 0.85 beats 0.60 in 26/32, with all four worlds agreeing on the latter direction. “Depth couples this skimmer body to the deep litter basin” is fair.

Two extrapolations are not:

1. O has reproduction disabled and observes cold-founded clones for 4,500 s; F observed descendants for 150 simulated minutes. O supplies a plausible mechanism for why the sign can differ, but it does not identify the cause of F's 84% descendant association. The report's “real only for a body that is not parked in a pool” reconciliation is too categorical (`ecology-v1-depth-factorial-2026-09-16.md:315-343,443-468`). F's census with only roster depth changed is still required.
2. The current wet floor has no producer: the plant step grows foliage only on living wood, while `algae_light` changes a living cell's light floor. O's 0.10/0.60 skimmer receives 4.66 m litter, earns 1.15 m credited material against a 4.67 e bill, and starves 32/32 (`ecology-v1-depth-factorial-2026-09-16.md:347-379,430-439`). This answers the **current skimmer/current parameters/fast-leaf** detrital-funding question negatively. It does not show that no body or calibrated detrital channel can be funded, so “one working guild” is premature (`ecology-v1-depth-factorial-2026-09-16.md:487-505`).

My advice to Wrysk is: do not add a wet-floor producer now. First run F's lineage census with only skimmer `depth = 0.55`. If the skimmer lineage persists without unacceptable loss of the other kinds, approve 0.55 as a correction to this roster. A wet producer would add a new stock, food web, and visible layer; it is a separate ecology design, not the repair required to interpret O.

## Next steps: Astra's opinion

### 1. Run the apex predicate pair — the single most informative cheap experiment

This remains Fable's best first item. It is a roughly 30-second, one-variable test of a measured rule that suppresses 91% of paid bursts. Compare current `inside` with `ContactMeasure::in_contact()` on identical seeds and introductions. Record held fraction, initial gap, relative closure, realised translation and turn consumption, contact/capture class, captures per life, phase occupancy, and K's credited/billed/net energy. The diagnosis is confirmed if the corrected arm delivers the burst, closes the gap, and increases contacts/captures; it is refuted if the held fraction falls but closure and contact do not improve. My owner advice: if it delivers those outcomes without a worse ledger, correct the predicate to its own comment; tune the escape multiple or strike timing only afterward.

### 2. Finish L with the data it actually lacks, not the score-spread task

Do the retained antithetic-pair reduction now; it requires no simulation. Then, only if needed, reconstruct generation 9's 32 candidates and run H's residence trace on the four training layouts. The quantity to explain is the signed plus/minus association among score, on-food fraction, dwell, and the resulting centred-rank update. Separately measure how often a \(\sigma\)-scale perturbation moves raw thrust or turn across the adapter deadbands. Keep the existing score. A score intervention returns to the table only if candidates with better sustained energy residence fail to rank better under the actual four-layout aggregation—not merely because scripted navigators reveal a large remote gradient.

### 3. Test the roster depth in the reproducing census; defer a wet producer

Run F's six-seed, 150-minute census with the skimmer's `depth` changed from 0.10 to 0.55 and every other genome/config value fixed. Measure founder survival and brood, descendant census by form × diet bin × guild, net margin, water-depth distribution, and effect on the other kinds. Confirm the roster correction if the skimmer establishes a lineage across seeds without replacing the world with a new monoculture; refute it if the founding rescue disappears under reproduction or harms variety. My owner advice: conditionally approve the roster depth change on that result. Do not bundle it with a wet-floor producer.

### 4. Treat §11 as a coupled-opening experiment, not a local-equilibrium fit

Before changing the contract, save plant-only states at several ages spanning the first crossing through 180,000 ticks and measure moving-window changes in total and per-cell \(P,W,Q,N\), exact plant income/loss, threshold crossings, and spatial variance. Found identical rosters into status quo and a small number of those whole-field states; provide Wrysk the opening frames and early founder outcomes. My owner advice: choose neither A nor B today. If preconditioning produces an honest, legible opening and improves the ecological experiment, choose A and specify the deterministic seed/config-bound preconditioning procedure in §11. Do not name a universal \((L\mu)_\mathrm{crit}\), and do not call a fitted tick-180,000 endpoint an equilibrium.

The display consequence must be part of that decision: a preconditioned world begins heterogeneous and substantially greener overall, so the current visible green-to-dim succession disappears. The counter should separately report exact-consumer crossings and ungrazed opening-stock declines under either choice.

### 5. Calibrate detrital funding only after the niche decision

O justifies a bounded feasibility surface, not a conclusion that detritus can never fund a guild. On a fixed body and fixed habitat/control, vary one declared detrital capacity or energy term at a time and measure the ledger chain `served litter → digestible → credited usable energy → whole bill`, plus substrate persistence. The first gate is non-negative median net margin in all or nearly all seed/world replicates without exhausting litter faster than §4 replenishes it. Keep this separate from the roster-depth decision and from adding a wet producer.

A, F, and I should receive short errata stating that their counter crossings at the shipped price were opening-stock plant-budget declines with zero measured withdrawal, not grazing depletion. This is document hygiene supported by M, not another experiment, and should precede future citations.

Relative to Fable's order: keep the apex pair first; replace item 4's already-answered score-spread task with the antithetic/update and local-residence analysis; keep the depth census ahead of any roster decision; replace the per-cell equilibrium fit with a whole-field preconditioning comparison; retain detrital calibration as later work; and do the errata immediately rather than treating them as the sixth research campaign.
