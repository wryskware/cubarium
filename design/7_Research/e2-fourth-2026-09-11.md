## Reading

- Locality is the lever. At speed 0.3 px/s with persistent turning (noise 0.6)
  and growth 0.008, the producer field is patchy (correlation length 3.3–3.5
  cells, spatial CV 0.34–0.37), a third of cells are above a quarter of
  `P_max` at any time, each cell is grazed down about twice in six hours and
  recovers after a median of about an hour, the population cycles between
  roughly 20 and 180 (CV 0.3) with no extinction in four seeds, and the
  per-face populations are only weakly synchronous (0.2–0.3). This is the
  first configuration showing repeated local depletion and recovery.
- Speed 1.2 px/s returns to the uniform lawn regardless of the other axes;
  0.6 is intermediate.
- Growth 0.004 with persistent slow movers crashes globally (face synchrony
  0.9, extinction in every seed); with diffusive turning (noise 1.5) it
  survives at low numbers with fewer patches.
- Diffusive turning stabilizes populations but weakens patchiness; persistent
  turning gives more structure and larger cycles.
- Cells reaching half of `P_max` remain rare everywhere: the standing crop
  peaks well below the logistic ceiling, so `P_max` remains mostly a formality.

## Adopted (leaning)

Defaults move to speed 0.4 px/s, sensing 6 px, turn noise 0.6, growth 0.008,
pending the 24-hour confirmation batch and viewing on the cube. Whether
0.3–0.4 px/s reads as alive at the display is a visual question for M3 gaits
and the physical review, not something this batch can answer.
