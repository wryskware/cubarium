# Ecology v1 round 4 — independent review

**Latest status — after repair 2: cleared.** The consolidated scope now distinguishes the predicate's measured burst suppression from the exploratory capture gain, uses E's 11.1 → 18.5 % and 18.5 → 27.0 % usable-energy ratios, limits Q to exact update reproduction and no geometric cancellation, gives the measured residence gap as about 18×, and leaves estimator variance, pair count, rank reduction and deadband causality unproved (`ecology-v1-round4-results-2026-09-16.md:407-434`). It also says “no tested age,” situates the 48,000-tick benefit in a greener still-transient coupled field, keeps T's arm confound explicit, and asks about a coupled grazed opening rather than a uniform total. The earlier P prose and S heading now agree with those qualifications (`ecology-v1-round4-results-2026-09-16.md:173-179,240-245`), and Q has one coherent corrected title above its correction block (`ecology-v1-es-antithetic-2026-09-16.md:7-34`). No P-level residual remains from this review cycle. The original findings below remain as history.

Disposition: retain P's intervention as evidence that the shipped pursuit predicate suppresses a paid lunge, retain R's refutation of `skimmer.depth = 0.55`, and retain S's comparison as evidence against a plant-only opening. T implements the motor approximation Wrysk requested and its paired whole-world result is useful. I would nevertheless **not deploy predicate and motor as one change from this package**. The predicate is a narrow source correction supported by an independent four-seed extension and should be adopted separately. The motor is a world-wide contract change whose apex arm also changes the prey world, whose production-host seam is not implemented, and whose own note says the selected calibration must be rerun. Q's exact replay and antithetic accounting establish implementation fidelity and no excess geometric cancellation; they do not rule out estimator variance, pair count, rank reduction, or the optimiser generally, and 5.5% versus 96.9% residence is about 18-fold rather than two orders of magnitude. S's 48,000-tick founder benefit is real but is the benefit of a much greener, still-transient coupled state, not a stationary §11 initializer. My owner advice is: approve the reach-envelope predicate; keep Sweep pending the grasp-only isolation below; make no roster-depth or §11 change; and test a turn-deadband-only training protocol without changing the score or aggregation.

## Findings

### P1 — Q proves faithful execution, not that the optimiser and pair count are ruled out

The reduction itself is valid for the question it actually computes. It re-derives all 512 candidate-layout scores, uses the trainer's utility, perturbation, gradient and Adam implementations, reproduces every recorded gradient norm, and replays all sixteen updates to `max |Δθ| = 0` (`ecology-v1-es-antithetic-2026-09-16.md:56-89`). The orthogonal reference is also the right null for the narrow question “did these sixteen nearly orthogonal high-dimensional pair contributions cancel more than vector geometry predicts?” Its 0.983–1.014 ratio supports **no excess geometric cancellation** (`ecology-v1-es-antithetic-2026-09-16.md:95-118,182-193`).

The report then makes a larger inference that the measurement cannot carry. Exact replay proves that the implementation applied its estimated update; it does not prove that the estimate is low-variance, that sixteen antithetic pairs are enough in 10,215 dimensions, that centred ranks preserve enough useful magnitude, or that Adam's operating point is adequate. The centre being in its sampled population's top quartile for nine generations is encouraging local evidence, but the centre score is noisy and ends below its generation-9 value; the reported mean improvement is only about 50 ticks per generation (`ecology-v1-es-antithetic-2026-09-16.md:205-221`). No independent gradient, split-half cosine, bootstrap stability, repeated training seed, or alternative pair count was measured. Therefore “refuted as an optimiser/update problem” and “the rank reduction and pair count are ruled out” are stronger than the evidence (`ecology-v1-es-antithetic-2026-09-16.md:275-301,315-335`; consolidated result:78-107).

The scale claim also does not check arithmetically. Generation 9 spans 0.76%–5.48% opening residence and the route control holds 96.9% (`ecology-v1-es-antithetic-2026-09-16.md:223-238`). Comparing the best observed candidate with the route control is (96.9/5.48 = 17.7), not approximately 100. The estimated 580 generations to close the score gap is a slow extrapolated rate, not “two orders of magnitude” in the measured residence quantity. The safe conclusion is: **candidate variation is real, useful residence is still small, the recorded update is reproduced exactly, and correlated-direction cancellation is not the loss**.

The deadband measurement identifies a plausible adapter loss, not its causal sufficiency. The reset turn channel is exactly zero on every sampled observation for 29/32 candidates and the carried centre is clipped on 42% of samples, with mean raw head 0.060 at a 0.050 edge (`ecology-v1-es-antithetic-2026-09-16.md:240-270`). But carried deadband occupancy correlates only (r=0.206) with score, thrust is continuously about 0.52 and never enters its stopping deadband, and no trajectory was run with a different turn band (`ecology-v1-es-antithetic-2026-09-16.md:255-273,343-357`). “The measured place weights lose their effect” should be “a measured clipping site and the leading adapter hypothesis.”

The four-layout minimum's 29% disagreement with the mean is real; calling it information loss or a second defect is a design judgement. `min` intentionally asks for worst-layout robustness. A candidate winning three layouts and losing badly on one is not misranked under that objective. Do not combine `Aggregate::Mean` with the deadband change in the next run: that would make a failed or successful campaign uninterpretable.

My bounded training change would be `TURN_DEADBAND: 0.05 -> 0.0` only, with `Aggregate::Min`, sigma, pair count, score, layouts and train seed fixed and a new protocol hash. Before a full campaign, replay the frozen generation-9 centre and candidates on their own layouts under both adapters, not merely on saved observations. The intervention is falsified as the current behavioural bottleneck if turn activity rises but on-food fraction, opening dwell and `t_min` do not. If it passes that check, run the same fixed-length 16-generation A/B. Separately, bootstrap or split the retained sixteen pair contributions and report gradient-direction stability; that is the cheap missing test before declaring pair count or the estimator adequate.

### P1 — The proposed cube deployment has no production motor/predicate contract and precedes the recalibration T says is required

Both switches are currently research transients. `PursuitStop` is set only by `apex-audit`; Graft's exhaustive symbol index finds no production caller of `World::set_pursuit_stop`. `MotorModel` is set in search fixtures, `evaluate`, and `apex_audit`, while a snapshot deliberately resumes as Sweep (`ecology-v1-motor-inertial-2026-09-16.md:110-127,137-155`; `crates/cubarium-search/src/evaluate.rs:440-455`). The host's neural seeding path checks `PolicyFile::policy()` and `check_ecology`, then attaches the policy, but it neither calls `check_motor` nor selects a motor model (`crates/cubarium/src/runner/mod.rs:245-288`). The only host diff in this round supplies `MotorModel::Sweep` to test policy constructors. Thus an inertial policy could presently be accepted by the host and run under Sweep, while a resumed inertial world also silently returns to Sweep unless future production code reapplies it.

Reading a missing policy `motor` as Sweep is sound backward compatibility **inside the search tools**: there was only one prior contract, the default protocol hash remains fixed, an inertial protocol hashes differently, and the focused provenance tests pass (`crates/cubarium-search/src/es/export.rs:28-44,109-131`; `ecology-v1-motor-inertial-2026-09-16.md:119-127,152-155`). It is not yet an end-to-end host provenance rule. Before either switch is visible on the cube, adoption needs an explicit production default or runtime contract, reapplied after snapshot load, exposed in status/provenance, and `seed_neural_animals` must call `check_motor` against the effective world just as it calls `check_ecology`.

Fable also reverses T's own dependency. T says all fifteen-candidate screen rows and the selected held-out validation must be rerun before `fast-leaf` can still be called the selected ecology under Inertial (`ecology-v1-motor-inertial-2026-09-16.md:444-461`). The consolidated recommendation instead deploys first and makes those rows the later baseline (`ecology-v1-round4-results-2026-09-16.md:427-439`). Thirty-six rows on only `baseline` and `fast-leaf` show that those two retain their existing gates; they do not show that the post-change search would still select `fast-leaf` from fifteen candidates. “No trained policy is installed” removes today's retraining migration, not the ecological selection obligation or the host seam.

I would not couple these decisions. The predicate changes one semantically wrong hold test and has its own paired evidence. Inertial changes every body's envelope and rotation price, produces 5.6% fewer animals in selected fast-leaf arm 0, and changes range and motor share in every one of 18 fast-leaf pairs (`ecology-v1-motor-inertial-2026-09-16.md:293-330`). Approve the predicate separately. Treat Inertial as an experimental candidate until the grasp-only isolation, production contract, and recalibration are complete.

### P2 — P is supported as an exploratory correction after the disclosed widening; it is not an eight-seed pre-registered confirmation

The four-seed registered pair is not confirmation of the contact limb by itself: contacts are 46→46, captures 15→21, and the gap improves but still grows. P explicitly discloses that it widened after seeing that flat contact result (`ecology-v1-apex-predicate-2026-09-16.md:343-357`). Pooling all eight after that decision would be post-selection.

The four added seeds are nevertheless a legitimate independent replication cohort if read separately, and they are unusually coherent: contacts 42→94, captures 23→46, and mean gap changes from growing by 1.23 px to closing by 0.05 px. The eight-seed gap-bin table also answers the “prey already in the claws” concern: under the shipped predicate all captures begin inside 8 px, while the corrected arm creates 17 contacts and 9 captures from 8–12 px (`ecology-v1-apex-predicate-2026-09-16.md:218-237`). My reading is therefore: the intervention establishes that the half-space suppresses the paid burst; capture improvement is replicated exploratorily; the pooled percentage is descriptive. This is enough to correct a predicate that contradicts its own reach-envelope comment, but not to present the apex as healthy. Every member still starves and no P member reaches its reproduction age (`ecology-v1-apex-predicate-2026-09-16.md:246-264,325-336`).

### P2 — S's founder benefit is real, but neither “only foliage” nor a uniform grazed-total initializer follows

The field and founding machinery is well checked. Age 0 reproduces M on all 12 rows, the saved/founded state agrees on all 48 arms, the plant identity closes, and the plant-only arm reproduces M's crossings (`ecology-v1-precondition-2026-09-16.md:57-91`). I inspected the contact sheet: it supports the claimed visible direction—status quo opens sparse and greens, while preconditioned columns open with a dense wet band and then thin (`ecology-v1-precondition-2026-09-16.md:273-302`).

The 48,000-tick effect is also real within this treatment: every grazer founder breeds at the 3,001-tick floor in all six seeds under both configurations, versus 0.3/10 baseline and 3/10 fast-leaf at status quo (`ecology-v1-precondition-2026-09-16.md:141-177`). But the prefix did not “only add foliage.” It evolved the whole coupled plant field—wood, reserve and nutrient as well as foliage—and then exposed founders to a much larger downward transient. Baseline opens at ΣP 358 rather than 107 and reaches a minimum 192 after 9,700 ticks; all arms later approach 215–230 (`ecology-v1-precondition-2026-09-16.md:247-271`). The dietary split makes food availability the leading explanation, but does not separate opening stock, nutrient history, plant reserve, and the transient. The result is “this preconditioned opening gives foliage feeders an immediate brood,” not “a stationary opening fixes the founders.”

The recycling mechanism is plausible and better supported than in M: after 180,000 plant-only ticks, 63.5 baseline cells are already below a quarter of their §11 stock, versus 11.2 with animals, and crossing-cell nutrient differs sharply (`ecology-v1-precondition-2026-09-16.md:212-240`). Still, removing animals jointly removes recycling, grazing, carcasses, movement and their spatial history. “Animal presence has a net preserving effect, consistent with recycling” is measured; recycling alone was not intervened on. Likewise, “no tested age removes crossings” is accurate (`ecology-v1-precondition-2026-09-16.md:304-318`); “no age” without that qualifier is not.

S correctly redirects attention from an ungrazed plant endpoint to a coupled grazed state, but ΣP 215–230 is not itself an initializer. The terminal fields remain spatial, nutrient-coupled and history-dependent. Fable's proposed “equivalent `initial_fraction`, uniform” arm (`ecology-v1-round4-results-2026-09-16.md:445-452`) can reproduce the total while recreating exactly the local over/under-seeding problem M exposed. I would tell Wrysk to leave §11 unchanged. The next comparison should use conservation-accounted snapshots of the **full coupled field** after an ordinary roster has produced the observed grazed state, then remove that burn-in population and found an identical fresh roster, versus status quo and the 48,000 plant-only opening. Measure per-cell (P,W,Q,N), the first-hour signed transient, founder broods and exact-withdrawal crossings. Do not write a scalar 215–230 target into §11.

### P2 — T is faithful to the requested approximation, but arm A does not isolate the motor's apex effect

The implementation matches the brief's declared model: physical extent supplies the disc radius, `r·|ω|/sqrt(2)` is the rotational energy-equivalent speed, the capability envelope is quadratic, the purse prices the linear sum, and the grasp is excluded (`ecology-v1-motor-inertial-2026-09-16.md:37-59`; `crates/cubarium-core/src/motor.rs:189-227,508-540,616-653`). The two-constraint resolver is internally coherent with that approximation and preserves pure translation. It is not a full “mass and momentum” physics model—the bill remains a linear movement proxy and acceleration is absent—but T says so. The untouched neural adapter is safe and conservative, not faithful to the new quadratic opportunity: it cannot request the diagonal the envelope would allow (`ecology-v1-motor-inertial-2026-09-16.md:478-495`).

Byte identity is adequately demonstrated for Sweep: hashes were captured before `MotorModel` existed, six boundaries are pinned, pure translation is pinned, and all 36 Sweep whole-world rows reproduce retained state hashes (`ecology-v1-motor-inertial-2026-09-16.md:137-160,278-284`). The ordinary-world effect is also clear rather than noise on fast-leaf: motor share, billed motor, range and feeding fraction move in the same direction in all 18 pairs; population arm 0 falls 60.8→56.0 while all six gates remain (`ecology-v1-motor-inertial-2026-09-16.md:286-330`).

Arm A does not separate those world changes from the apex motor. At introduction, the Inertial worlds contain 628 prey versus 745 under Sweep, a 15.7% difference, and every prey has had the new motor from tick 0 (`ecology-v1-motor-inertial-2026-09-16.md:205-211,257-260`). The apex nonetheless captures more absolute prey in a thinner world, so the direction is compelling. The magnitude—contacts +48%, captures +39%, lifetime maximum doubled—and attribution between disc envelope, omitted grasp, and changed prey dynamics are not isolated. “The reproduction age gate opens” means two members can pass the age threshold; it is not reproduction, readiness, or viability. Reserve is still the first refusing term and all 32 starve (`ecology-v1-motor-inertial-2026-09-16.md:249-255`).

This is why T's grasp-only intermediate is the honest next experiment, and why I prefer it to Fable's bundle. Under Sweep, change only the apex radius from `max(lobes, grasp)` to lobes while leaving every ordinary body and prey trajectory unchanged. If that arm recovers most of Inertial's closure, capture and lifetime gain, fix the geometry without changing the world's motor contract. If it does not, the residual is evidence for the quadrature/disc model rather than for merely removing a claw from the body's moment arm.

## Next steps: Astra's opinion

### 1. Run the Sweep grasp-only apex pair — the single most informative cheap experiment now

Use P's corrected reach-envelope predicate, eight held-out seeds and the same two configurations. Compare shipped Sweep with a variant whose apex `turn_radius_px` uses lobe extent rather than `capture_offset + capture_reach`; ordinary bodies remain bit-identical. Record prey population at introduction, delivered translation/rotation, signed gap closure, contacts/captures by initial gap, usable ledger fraction, lifetime and readiness terms.

Confirmation that geometry is the principal apex motor defect: the variant preserves the identical prey world and recovers most of Inertial's −0.57 px/burst closure and 2.91 captures/life. Refutation: it remains near Sweep's +0.21 px/burst and 2.09 captures/life. This approximately one-minute arm separates a narrow grasp correction from a world-wide motor contract and is more decision-relevant than deploying both together.

### 2. Adopt the reach-envelope predicate separately; do not call the apex healthy

My advice to Wrysk: **yes on the predicate**. The half-space contradicts the stated reach meaning, suppresses 89% of paid bursts, and the independent four added seeds reproduce the direction. Make reach-envelope the production rule, add a production default/resume regression, and keep the research switch only if useful. Do not change escape, strike duration, sense or mating radius with it. Expected visible result: an introduced apex charges and handles prey more often. Expected ecological result: still a starving introduced body, not a reproducing lineage.

### 3. Decide the motor after the isolation and production/calibration work

My advice to Wrysk: **the Inertial model is a faithful implementation of the approximation you requested, but do not put it on the cube from this evidence yet**. If the grasp-only pair captures most of the benefit, take that correction and leave Sweep. If it does not and the disc/quadrature model remains the desired world physics, then implement the host selection/resume/status and policy check, adapt the action request to the quadratic envelope, rerun the fifteen-candidate screen and held-out selection, and only then start a fresh cube world. The current live world should not be migrated: both research switches are transient, and a new motor changes the task and population.

Acceleration cost is not next. First establish the memoryless model's contract and its selected ecology; adding momentum before that would introduce another visible variable.

### 4. Test the training adapter alone, and leave the score/minimum alone

My advice to Wrysk: **no score change**. Test turn deadband 0.05 versus 0.0 under a new protocol with `Aggregate::Min`, sigma, pair count, layouts and score fixed. First do trajectory replays of the centre/candidates; proceed to a bounded 16-generation pair only if turn release raises on-food residence or `t_min`. Falsify the adapter diagnosis if turns become expressible without improving residence and worst-layout survival. Measure bootstrap/split-half gradient direction from Q's retained pairs before declaring sixteen pairs sufficient. Treat the 29% min/mean disagreement as the price of robustness, not a bug, unless Wrysk explicitly changes the training objective.

### 5. Run the skimmer depth ladder; add no wet-floor producer yet

My advice to Wrysk: **no roster change at 0.55**. Run depth {0.10, 0.20, 0.30, 0.40, 0.55, 0.75}, arm 0, with founder/descendant ledger bins and served foliage/litter per body. Accept a roster depth only if a lineage persists across seeds without materially reducing the grazer. If no such depth exists, the evidence becomes a real choice between three viable heights/four kinds and designing a wet-floor producer. That producer is a new food web and display layer, not a repair to a locus.

### 6. Leave §11; measure a coupled opening rather than a uniform total

My advice to Wrysk: **no §11 or preconditioning change now**. The 48,000 plant-only opening makes founders breed but produces a larger reverse transient; the status quo produces a smaller forward transient. A uniform `initial_fraction` chosen to total 215–230 throws away the per-cell field that generated that standing crop. Compare full, conservation-accounted coupled-field snapshots as described above, with opening and one-hour frames, exact plant budgets and identical fresh rosters. If a stable-looking coupled initializer exists and keeps the founder benefit without the crash, then decide its visible succession deliberately. Until then the current thin-green opening is the honest default.

### 7. Repair the record before the next owner decision

Correct Q's scope and 18× arithmetic; correct P/T's ledger units and percentages; qualify S's “only foliage,” “recycling,” and “no age” wording; and replace the consolidated deploy-first recommendation with the separated order above. These are evidence repairs, not new experiments.

Relative to Fable's order: split item 1 into predicate adoption, grasp-only isolation, and conditional motor adoption; put the grasp-only pair first. Keep the depth ladder, but after the apex decision. Replace the uniform grazed-total arm with a full coupled-field comparison. Keep adapter work, but change only the deadband and measure estimator stability; do not pair it with `Aggregate::Mean`. Defer acceleration and reserve-readiness work until the motor contract is chosen.
