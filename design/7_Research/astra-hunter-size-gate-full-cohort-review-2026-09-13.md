# Size-aware hunter growth: complete two-hour cohort

**Reject size-gate-only as a rollout/longer-run candidate.** It unlocks correctly
paid juvenile growth, but all15 candidate offspring stop below46% of adult
structure and starve. There is still no adult descendant or descendant reproduction,
founder persistence declines overall, and seed2 facultative-on loses a prey form the
reference retains. Keep the implementation/evidence as a tested mechanism, not a
solution for sustainable Lanternjaw recruitment. No24/72-hour extension, new recipe,
live hunter introduction or ordinary-fauna change follows.

There are real benefits and mixed cases: seed8's specialist founder lives much
longer and reproduces more, and several prey outcomes improve. Those are retained
below; neither marginal resource counters nor changed ancestry alone establish cause.
The selected Fable creature/silhouette is unchanged by this biological conclusion.

## Paid development, not adult recruitment

Reference:18 children, no growth,17 starvation deaths plus one right-censored
juvenile in seed2 facultative-on. Candidate:15 children, **all grow/all starve**,
no censoring. All remain below adult targetS=2: maximumS ranges
**.8485–.9081** (42.425–45.405% of adult), versus birthS=.8.
All growth occurs in the **first1200-tick/60-second flow bin** of each child;
none grows in a later bin. Every child has485–1081 positive growth steps, only
24.25–54.05 s of active growth versus the600 s needed to build1.2 at the fixed
.002/s rate. Those steps need not be consecutive; bins do not give the exact final
positive step. Children live223.1–890.85 s, not merely a few unfinished display frames.

Across the15 children:9,430 growth steps build **.943 material**, spend the same
reserve, debit **.4715 battery**, and emit **2.3575 construction heat**. Every
positive increment is rate-limited; reserve/energy/final-size caps do not bind any
recorded positive increment. Exactly9,430 of128,523 gate observations have reserve
above the moving threshold and enter growth. Thus growth **permission closes**
for the remaining observations; it is not a case of an open gate repeatedly failing
the inner battery cap. Maximum/adult metadata at the last increment was fixed before
this run, so zero adulthood is not the pilot's old observation bug.

The gate is not the only resource story. Whole-lifetime candidate-child reserve:

```text
12.0000 birth reserve + 6.611180 actual assimilated intake
  = .943000 growth + 17.668180 oxidation + 0 reserve at death
```

Three children take **no reserve intake at all**, yet grow from paid parental
endowment; six never digest prey. “All grew” is not successful acquisition.
Oxidation consumes94.93% of available reserve, but supplies real energy:

```text
9.0000 birth battery + 5.232361 field/digestion energy + 28.269089 oxidation energy
  = 34.505549 paid upkeep + 7.280000 strikes + .244400 handling
    + .471500 construction + 0 battery at death
```

These recorded balances close within the existing tolerance. Upkeep demand includes
15.42276 sensing,13.86641 maintenance and5.21886 movement; the paid lump is slightly
lower at terminal exhaustion. No marginal component is independently “unnecessary.”
Only.627822 of17.668180 oxidized reserve is recorded above the old configured
threshold. That does **not** mean removing charge80 would preserve exactly.627822:
energy, survival, pursuit and food intake respond. Nor does banning oxidation turn
its28.269 battery contribution into free construction resources.

## Prey diversity and recovery

The clearest new loss is **seed2 facultative form1 (glider)**: reference closes
with19, candidate reaches zero at220260 and closes zero. Total closing prey remains
108 in both because candidate has more other forms (77/0/31 versus60/19/29).
Total population is therefore not an adequate diversity check.

Other matched form sets agree. Seed1 form3 is lost in both roles/recipes, earlier
in candidate185868 versus reference188166; seed3 specialist form3 loss228203 is
identical. Forms absent at opening are not newly extinct. Specialist pooled prey-time
increases~.269% but closing prey1215→1213; facultative prey-time falls~.109% with
closing1217→1220. Net surviving opening-prey ancestry counts250→256 and245→255 do
not cancel the form loss or prove genome diversity; no cross-world individual
identity match is assumed after divergence.

Local recovery records retain different capture windows, not matched per-capture
causal trials. Reference specialist/facultative:28/36 recovered,3/4 unrecovered by
3600 s,4/5 right-censored,11/11 insufficient prehistory,1/6 no measured deficit.
Candidate:29/38 recovered,1/5 unrecovered,0/0 right-censored,11/11 insufficient,
2/5 no measured deficit. These totals do not establish improved recovery: windows,
capture recurrence, timing and populations differ. The whole-world form loss remains
material even beside a locally “recovered” count.

## Next distinct mechanistic question — existing records first

Ask **why juveniles never regain post-oxidation growth permission after their first
minute**, not “how far should we lower the same gate again?” The records already
show first-minute/endowment-funded growth, no later gate re-entry, near-total
reserve conversion to fuel, real intake heterogeneity and paid starvation. Next
bounded analysis can align these children's existing60 s resource bins with their
actual target/attempt/handling event histories: distinguish no-acquisition children,
small sporadic meals and costly unsuccessful pursuit; show whether any later food
episode produces net stored material after real fuel demand. Keep all15 children,
including zero-intake cases; no new run or instrumentation yet.

A precise limit: bins expose aggregate inflow/outflow and endpoint stocks, while
gate metadata exposes first/last/min/max/sums and open counts. They do **not** retain
the joint per-decision tuple `(R before oxidation, actual burn, R after oxidation,
current S/gate, E before growth, all caps)` at the exact final/reopening threshold
crossing. Thus they cannot uniquely attribute that crossing to same-tick oxidation
versus prior intake timing or quantify a causal cheaper-growth/charging alternative.
If the existing event/bin alignment cannot resolve the acquisition question, name
that exact missing joint record; do not launch a broader metabolism sweep or invent
counterfactual food. Root can choose the next step after this finite review.
