## Reading

- Eleven of twelve worlds lived the whole day with sustained cycles: population
  between roughly 15 and 190 (CV 0.3), a crash of more than half every 25–60
  minutes, about 350 births per hour, a patchy producer field (correlation
  length 2.7–3.6 cells, spatial CV 0.33–0.38), each cell grazed down every
  3–6 hours and recovering after a median of 65–100 minutes, and per-face
  synchrony of 0.1–0.4. This satisfies the E2 gate's requirement of repeated
  local feeding and detritus succession without the whole cube emptying, in
  several configurations and seeds.
- One world (0.4 px/s, moving weather, seed 1) went extinct after seven hours
  from a trough of the ordinary cycle. Troughs of 11–29 organisms are common,
  so extinction over days is a real risk of this ecology; the plan defers the
  response (paid dormancy and one logged reseed) to M4 and forbids any fallback
  during the M2/M3 gates. 0.3 px/s had deeper safety margins and is the
  adopted default.
- Moving weather made the field slightly patchier (correlation length 3.5
  versus 2.8 cells) and did not change survival.
- Mass residuals stayed below 3.1e-10 over 1.7 million ticks.
