3. **Important — The attempt categories support an arrival bottleneck, but do not prove deaths, causal failure reasons or a hard reach ceiling.** Evidence: `succession_study.rs:261–271,369–405,497–515,583–635`; `crates/cubarium-voxel-flora/src/seeds.rs:457–469,510–542`; `results:74–101`.
