# First complete quiet screen: real recovery, not everyday quiet yet

**Keep Off as the default.** The candidate supplies genuine two-second post-birth
recovery, but it is too sparse to establish the requested everyday quiet habits.
The no-care comparison is modestly adverse overall, with real identity-level
ancestry losses hidden by net counts. Recommend **one unchanged two-hour follow-up
as a bounded diagnostic**, not deployment, a passed biological gate, or automatic
24/72-hour escalation. A more regularly readable habit needs a separate mechanism;
do not lengthen this timer or loosen its affordability rule to make it look busier.

No parameters, core, art, runner, live process or original artifact changed here.
This interpretation covers every seed and all48 arms, not selected examples.

The3–16 bouts per no-care seed correspond to one event per37.5–200 seconds on a

life-event gesture, not a recurring everyday rhythm for most organisms. Two seconds
can be legible while following one parent, but **no rendering or physical-display
observation is supplied by these numeric artifacts**. Passing the proposal's9-of-12
screen (actual12-of-12) proves opportunity exists, not adequate visual prevalence.

2.897px no-care and2.800px fed across all twelve worlds. No pause is perfectly
motionless by fiat, no satiation is fabricated, and no energy gain is implied.

### Net ancestry counts conceal paired losses

The reducer's `paired_losses.lost_opening_cohorts` computes
`max(0, Off_count − candidate_count)`. Its6 no-care/5 fed flags are **net deficits**,
not an exhaustive identity-level comparison. Reconstructing live ancestry from each
opening and the complete life stream finds **28 Off-only cohorts across10/12 no-care
seeds**, offset by24 candidate-only cohorts. Fed:27 Off-only across**12/12** seeds,
offset by26 candidate-only. Even a net gain can therefore lose ancestry the reference
retained. This does not make the technical reduction invalid; it limits its label
and makes the proposed biological acceptance condition materially stricter than
those six/five flags suggest. Root should retain both set differences in future
interpretations. An opening cohort is an identity-based ancestry bucket, not a
claim of distinct genomes, species, or independent ancient founders.

**Identity turnover is not automatically global biological harm.** Both directions
are substantial, as expected when a behavioral intervention changes trajectories.
The owner's objective is viable, diverse unattended ecology—not preserving every
matched individual or ancestry forever. These sets expose what the proposal's
wording asks us to inspect; they do not override that objective. The main reasons
not to promote are the still-unproven everyday benefit, short horizon, and combined
population/reproduction signals, not the mere existence of a reference-only ID.

There is no total extinction, and direct closing **form-set** comparison finds no
reference-only form in any pair—not merely matching form counts. Animals born
during the run and still alive at close number401→388 no-care and396→386 fed.
This is short-term offspring survival, strongly affected by different birth timing;
it is not mature recruitment, fecundity, or a longer-horizon viability test.

## Attribution and measurement limits

Skipping intake/budding while still paying real maintenance/sensing/growth is a
plausible opportunity cost; the affordability check is immediate reserve/energy
coverage, not a long-run fitness guarantee. But the outputs do **not** identify
missed food amounts, exact ordinary escrow funding/oxidation, or individual causal
debits. Intake-tick totals change−0.0965% no-care/−0.2320% fed; total path changes
−0.5390%/−0.3722%. These include divergent populations/locations/food competition,
so neither is a direct measure of savings caused by resting. Deaths and stochastic
trajectory differences must not be explained away, nor causally assigned without
additional evidence.

Population-time, intake and transported path cover **post-step survivors**. An
organism dying during a step contributes no terminal living-time/path sample. Held
death intervals have a separate bout/death counter, zero in this screen. This keeps
the reported denominators consistent but does not recover every pre-step body's
terminal intake or movement. Ten minutes and twelve seeds establish neither
universal noninferiority nor stability across generations.

## Next action

1. Retain this preliminary, non-promoted biological result. Permit at most **one unchanged
   two-hour, all12×4-arm comparison from the same original openings** as a diagnostic
   of whether the adverse differences grow or are short-window timing effects.
   Keep the recipe, admissions,40 ticks, costs, ambient support, care and strict
   audits unchanged. Do not pick successful seeds or retune between horizons.
2. Interpret ancestry by both identity-set differences, no-care survival/population
   time first, then timed child recruitment. The proposal's ancestry-loss condition
   is **not cleared**, nor should it be recast as an individual-preservation mandate;
   a longer diagnostic is not an authorization to deploy. Stop
   automatic24/72-hour progression and reassess after that finite run.
3. Separately scope an **affordable post-feeding-bout disengagement** candidate if
   the goal remains everyday habit. It needs a genuine bounded feeding episode,
   explicit rearming after new intake/activity, actual active-vs-already-resting
   evidence, resource checks, and a bounded missed-intake duty cycle. `fed=true`
   alone is not a meal amount or permission to pause every tick. Do not bundle it
   with this birth timer, change the satiation threshold, supply free energy, or
   display false rest over ongoing feeding. Choose its fixed rule only after the
   forthcoming ordinary feeding diagnostics; no new parameter values are selected
   in this review.
