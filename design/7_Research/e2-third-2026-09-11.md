## Reading

- No configuration met the E2 gate: the fraction of cells at or below a
  quarter of `P_max` stayed at 0.98–1.0, no cell reached half of `P_max`
  after burn-in, and the producer correlation length stayed near one cell.
- Saturating intake (`K_P = 0.45`) raised births per hour by about 50 % and
  spatial variation of the producer from 0.11 to 0.17, and made the per-face
  producer sums weakly synchronous (0.3–0.4) without population crashes.
- Habitat contrast tripled and weather amplitude doubled changed almost
  nothing: consumers equalize any spatial difference in regrowth.
- No extinctions and low population variation everywhere (CV 0.04–0.09):
  stable, but spatially homogeneous.
- Interpretation: at 0.75–1.5 px/s an organism crosses a face in about a
  minute, and producers regrow on a one-to-two-minute time scale at growth
  0.016, so consumers redistribute faster than any patch can recover. The
  cube behaves as one well-mixed patch. The next batch tests locality
  directly (much slower movement, more random turning, shorter sensing, slower
  regrowth) rather than more habitat structure.
