# Astra — minimal-gut implementation review, 2026-10-03

Reviewed `gut` at `69dc7851` in `.claude/worktrees/gut`, including the diff against main and the test changes in `70d50fd5` and `b32cd812`. Evidence paths and line numbers below refer to that checkout. Repair 2 overrides the earlier proposal. This is an implementation review, not an accepted ecological decision.

The core gut can land after the blocking defect and the control fixes below. The digestion cap, body-scaled bite defaults, proportional regurgitation, rider transfer, stored totals and serialization largely implement the spec. Search rebasing remains the explicitly separate item-11 package. The builder's test counts and world results are supplied evidence; I reviewed their fixtures and implementation but did not rerun those suites or launch ecological studies.

1. **Blocking — Zero-mineral food can permanently occupy stomach capacity.** **Evidence:** `crates/cubarium-voxel-fauna/src/step.rs:679–699`; `crates/cubarium-voxel-flora/src/cover.rs:2019–2049`; `crates/cubarium-voxel-flora/src/lib.rs:4651–4675,4766–4789,4841–4846`. Vine nectar **and fruit** return `mineral: 0.0`; vine foliage does too (`cover.rs:1999–2003`). With positive animal tissue mineral demand, their effective yield is zero and `digest` never removes them. This follows Repair 2 item 2 literally, but exposes a defect in that spec. A full stomach may first switch eating off; as reserves empty, appetite can switch it on again, yet there is no room for another food and the trapped pool still cannot digest. Mineral-bearing food of the **same class** can improve a partially filled pool's composition; another class cannot fund it. Bloomcrown nectar and stand fruit normally carry mineral but can also return none when stand mineral is exhausted. Glassfilm and carrion withdraw their stored mineral pro rata; ordinary funded film and tissue corpses are not intrinsically mineral-free, although these APIs can also withdraw a mineral-free pool.

   **Change:** replace indefinite holding at zero effective yield with an explicit, conservative passage/rejection path for unassimilable raw food, retaining the reserve-room cap for the assimilable portion. Do not invent mineral or bypass tissue funding. Separately, make the intended vine diet nutritionally viable through a funded producer-side mineral path if it is supposed to support these animals. Add short cases using real vine nectar/fruit, a depleted stand, and a mixed stomach; verify capacity becomes available and all three currencies close. Mark the necessary change to item 2 explicitly.

2. **Important — Bellwing failure is not explained by access alone, and the cadence fixture cannot establish that.** **Evidence:** `crates/cubarium-voxel-fauna/src/step.rs:1638–1676`; `crates/cubarium-voxel-fauna/src/flight_tests.rs:1135–1186`; `crates/cubarium-voxel-fauna/src/controller.rs:1038–1045`; `crates/cubarium/examples/founder_tally.rs:613–643`. The flyer path correctly checks eating, computes stomach room, withdraws nectar and calls `swallow`/`settle_full`. I found no missing full-event call there. The bloomcrown interval fixture deliberately supplies mineral-rich, reachable nectar; it is a valid controlled cadence fixture, not a test of the vine-only diet in the reported worlds. Vine nectar already failed to fund assimilation before this change; the new gut additionally retains it rather than processing it away. Conversely, **zero completed meals plus eating on continuously does not prove a full clogged stomach**: it can mean insufficient raw intake ever reached 0.98K. The teacher also now forces `resting=false`, so its old fed-perching behavior is removed, not merely delegated to the mouth gate.

   **Change:** fix finding 1 and add a small real-vine sip case. In the next bounded observation separate raw swallowed nectar, effective yield, digested nectar, S/K, reach failures and reserve change per bellwing. Treat a still-empty stomach as access/effort trouble and a mineral-free occupied stomach as the nutritional/passage problem. Do not tune the full threshold to make the meal counter pass. Preserve or explicitly replace fed perching using the new satiety state if the teacher is expected to rest between sips; the bloomcrown fixture uses a scripted perched feeder and does not cover that behavior.

3. **Important — Pack eligibility changed, but leader selection and prey sizing still count nonparticipants.** **Evidence:** `crates/cubarium-voxel-fauna/src/pack.rs:79–97,291–304,519–560,579–621`; `crates/cubarium-voxel-fauna/src/gut_tests.rs:605–658`. `pack_wants` implements the half-adults-or-emergency condition, and `joins` gates the current member's chase. But the nearest **any adult** becomes driver, including an adult whose latch is off and who will not execute that chase. The hungry adults then flank around a nonparticipating driver. Nearby-mate detection and the pack body window likewise count adults without applying participation. This can authorize prey that the actual hunters cannot handle. The 222 chases at roughly 0.5% success are compatible with this inconsistency, but aggregate counts do not identify which failed chase had it. The test verifies target acquisition, not execution by the chosen participants.

   **Change:** derive one eligible, awake, nearby participant set and use it consistently for driver/flanker selection and participating body strength; retain the spec's separate pack-level appetite quorum. Add a short case where the nearest adult is fed, two others want food, and only those two drive/flank. The `spent` exception prevents the old reserve floor from immediately vetoing emergency hunters and is understandable, but removing that floor for every joining hungry member also removes their chase-exhaustion stop. Keep the timeout and document this deliberate behavior; a replacement stamina/abandonment policy belongs to predator-cadence tuning, not a silent claim that the old floor still operates.

4. **Important — A hungry pack can select another live target before returning to its existing carcass.** **Evidence:** `crates/cubarium-voxel-fauna/src/pack.rs:473–509,524–562,579–637`; `crates/cubarium-voxel-fauna/src/step.rs:1383–1410`. When a target disappears, the pack can record its feast, clear the target, then acquire a new target in the same steering call. Acquisition and chase return before the feast branch. A hungry member can therefore leave uneaten carrion for another prey. At the mouth, eligible live prey also takes precedence over a carrion bite. These paths predate parts of this package, but the gut now relies on actual carcass intake to switch hunting off.

   Regurgitation is a second, legitimate cause of renewed appetite: at the default rate 0.01 body/s, a chorister can transfer an empty-to-full stomach of 0.20 body in about **20 seconds**, if pups have enough room. Breeding, depleted arrival reserves and growth further invalidate comparing all two-hour kills with a resting, non-breeding adult's 1.3-day interval. Thus 1–12 kills, or 18 grazers falling to one, does not by itself demonstrate broken digestion. Nor does it establish that provisioning caused those kills. `pack_chase_kills` is itself a disappearance-with-carrion proxy (`pack.rs:477–484`), not proof this chase killed that target.

   **Change:** prioritize a usable existing feast before acquiring new prey and test that a hungry pack at its carcass does not immediately start another chase. Record food taken from each feast and why it was abandoned. Leave prey production, pack size, litter costs, pursuit success and sustainable kill-frequency targets to the predator-cadence package, using non-breeding and nursing packs separately. Do not add a one-kill-per-day timer to hide this control problem.

5. **Important — Emergency refill bouts are expected, but the on/off thresholds overlap near starvation.** **Evidence:** `crates/cubarium-voxel-fauna/src/body.rs:823–851`; `crates/cubarium-voxel-fauna/src/step.rs:477–481,543–598`; `crates/cubarium/examples/founder_tally.rs:638–669`. At h=0.9, appetite restarts at 0.83K; the full event stops at 0.98K. Repeated small refills while reserve remains low follow this model, and are not necessarily sleep flicker. At h≥**0.988235**, however, `s_start ≥ 0.98`: an animal just declared full can become eating again on the next tick without a meaningful stomach depletion. A subsequent tiny bite closes another recorded meal. At h=1 the start threshold is K. Neither emergency state nor its below-0.6 exit requirement makes a meal-length distinction here.

   **Change:** keep the wake latch separate, but give the eating latch a nonoverlapping start/full band, with a named, tunable minimum refill gap or an equivalent explicit rule. Cover a severely depleted reserve and S≈0.98K in a few ticks. Do not interpret 1,600–2,500 completed emergency bouts as that many independent emergencies: the tally aggregates bodies and counts full events, not emergency transitions. Report raw amount per bout, time between starts, open bouts and reserve recovery before deciding that the observed ~20-second bouts are either healthy or pathological. The snail's **+26%** is from a fixed adult, full-reserve carrion fixture with no growth; it is not evidence that the juvenile/emergency cycling caused that interval error. Phase-delayed feeding and the fixture's first three intervals can shift that mean.

6. **Important — The executable safety precedence omits attached and falling bodies.** **Evidence:** Repair 2 table at `design/metabolism-meals-2026-10-02.md:244–255`; `crates/cubarium-voxel-fauna/src/step.rs:483–496,719–733,1155–1164`. Alerts, flight, climbing, submersion, active strikes and emergency waking are handled. But `free` does not exclude `a.attached.is_some()` or `mobility.falling`. An attached rider may therefore enter ordinary off-phase sleep and pay sleep upkeep, contrary to the explicit attached-is-awake row. The act stage subsequently skips its motion without correcting that state. A falling non-flying body is similarly not explicitly protected by this predicate.

   **Change:** include the missing safety conditions in the resolved wake state and add short off-phase cases. Preserve the separate rule that attached riders cannot start mouth-feeding. The reported 1.179-day fixed-host result supports the new take rate, but does not waive precedence in a cycling world.

7. **Important — The reserve fallback gained an extra appetite restriction that item 6 does not specify.** **Evidence:** `crates/cubarium-voxel-fauna/src/pack.rs:184–204,231–277`; `design/metabolism-meals-2026-10-02.md:235–241`. Regurgitation correctly requires a wanting pup, moves all food pools by the same fraction, respects stomach room and ignores the giver's reserve floor. The fallback reuses the same `hungriest` helper, so it too requires `p.eating`. A pup with a full stomach and low reserve cannot receive the old reserve assistance even if the adult has an empty stomach and surplus reserve. This is a declared deviation, but “existing reserve-to-reserve transfer” did not introduce that extra condition.

   **Change:** use separate eligibility for the existing reserve fallback—pup reserve room and giver floor/efficiency—while keeping the eating requirement on raw regurgitation. Alternatively amend the spec explicitly to choose delayed assistance, and test that case rather than calling it unchanged. The revised old provisioning fixture starts stomachs empty, so it cannot distinguish these behaviors.

8. **Important — The books are substantive, but the tests no longer establish the exact digestion split.** **Evidence:** `crates/cubarium-voxel-fauna/src/step.rs:662–714,2410–2450`; `crates/cubarium-voxel-fauna/src/gut_tests.rs:197–210,864–898`; `crates/cubarium-voxel-fauna/tests/grazing.rs:194–220`; `crates/cubarium-voxel-fauna/tests/founder_tick.rs:279–288`. Digestion scales all candidate pool withdrawals to the available reserve room; it does not independently grant that room to every food. Removed pool composition goes through the existing assimilation/egesta/respiration settlement. Rider transfer debits host and credits rider in three currencies. Regurgitation debits one stomach and credits the other, without a second yield. I found no double counting on those normal paths.

   The day test is **not merely a tautology**: it sums actual stored state and compares it to boundary ledgers, exercising ingestion, digestion, regurgitation and riders. Its stomach equation also compares actual pools to recorded withdrawals. Nevertheless, it can pass with the wrong yield partition or wrong digestion speed if transfers remain conservative; it does not verify every spec invariant. The older room-cap test's exact reserve/egesta/unfunded-respiration assertions were replaced by a raw-stomach-fill test, and the exact 0.5-yield feedback assertion became “digested > 0”. Those checks belonged to an unchanged assimilation rule and need replacement, not deletion.

   **Change:** add a short independent numeric digestion test with two pools, deliberately small reserve room, one mineral-limited yield, and expected reserve/egesta/respiration/mineral/energy deltas. Verify no surplus respiration from overfilling. Add a direct flora-withdrawal-to-stomach boundary check using actual foods. Correct the `FaunaLedger` comment at `lib.rs:2442–2447`: `swallowed − digested − stomach_out` omits introduced stomach stock; the day test correctly uses its initial stock baseline, while later introductions need their own stomach input if this becomes a general diagnostic.

9. **Minor — Death and saved state are implemented; removal semantics and round-trip coverage need clarification.** **Evidence:** `crates/cubarium-voxel-fauna/src/step.rs:1938–1979`; `crates/cubarium-voxel-fauna/src/lib.rs:1166–1191,3387–3416,3486–3495`; `crates/cubarium-voxel-fauna/src/snapshot.rs:36–40,384–428`; `crates/cubarium-voxel-fauna/src/predation_life_tests.rs:524–532`. Stomach matter is included once in stored totals and raw corpse deposition, with mineral and energy preserved. Founding stock is booked as introduced matter. `Stomach`, eating/emergency latches and `Bout` are serialized, and schema 36 refuses older snapshots. Explicit removal instead books a conservative export of the whole animal; it does **not** deposit the stomach as carcass/litter, unlike item 10's literal removal wording. This retains the existing removal command's semantics and is reasonable if documented.

   **Change:** distinguish export/removal from death in the spec. Extend the small round-trip test to nonempty mixed pools, both latches set and an active nondefault bout, rather than mostly default new fields. Validate finite nonnegative pool currencies on loading as for existing body stocks. A newborn's empty stomach/off latch and a founder's s0 stomach/off latch otherwise match item 3; using tissue composition for synthetic founding food is acceptable as a booked endowment, not evidence that real food is nutritionally adequate.

10. **Important — The controlled cadence results are useful, but the acceptance evidence is narrower than the report implies.** **Evidence:** `crates/cubarium-voxel-fauna/src/gut_tests.rs:95–109,257–326`; `crates/cubarium-voxel-fauna/src/flight_tests.rs:1135–1186`; `design/metabolism-meals-2026-10-02.md:286–301`.     **Change:** label controlled-model cadence separately from food-access/ecological evidence. After fixing the defects, finish the missing distinct seeds if the full acceptance measure is being claimed; report the longer exploratory run separately. Do not make a long soak a prerequisite for correcting these demonstrable local defects.
