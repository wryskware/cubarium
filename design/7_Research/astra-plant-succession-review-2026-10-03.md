The target-restricted seed path is a sound first experiment for Wrysk's chosen succession direction. Keep velvetpad's current water budget. This mechanism permits succession on reachable, sufficiently bright, drained sites; it does not enforce a universal moss → grass → woody sequence, nor guarantee that replacing moss preserves food production. 1. **Important — Rate: 0.1 is a fast local replacement chance, not a two-day succession clock.** Evidence: `crates/cubarium-voxel-flora/src/seeds.rs:69–82,415–445,480–500`; `crates/cubarium-voxel-flora/src/lib.rs:2607`. The site is checked every 30 s. A bank must win the mass-weighted lottery, then win the overtopping draw to arm. At the next check it sprouts if still eligible, without another lottery or overtop draw. It does **not** need two successes at probability p. With one continuously eligible species, approximate mean waiting times are `30/p + 30` seconds of eligible checks: **5.5 min at 0.1, 17.2 min at 0.03, 50.5 min at 0.01**, plus arrival/check phase. Night, interrupted gates and competing banks lengthen these; a lottery share q approximately substitutes pq for p while conditions remain fixed. **Change:** start the study at **0.01**, with **0.03** as the faster comparison and zero as baseline. Keep woody 0.1 and the global check interval unchanged. Log seed arrival, eligible checks, lottery wins, arming, failed arms and establishment before increasing p. A 40–50% landscape takeover over two world days is a reasonable proposed visual target, not something these probabilities alone predict.

2. **Important — The target restriction must survive arming and preserve woody behavior.** Evidence: `crates/cubarium-voxel-flora/src/seeds.rs:403–425,488–499,459–473`; proposal `design/plant-succession-2026-10-03.md:45–57`. Today the occupied stand is reduced to a Boolean ground-cover flag. Introduce its species into candidate eligibility: positive overtop probability **and** an allowed current target **and** the normal gates and bank. Recompute that eligibility at the sprouting check so an arm cannot survive a change to a forbidden cover. Keep the final replacement guarded by the current target as well. **Change:** implement `overtop_covers = ["velvetpad"]` for springturf, with the documented empty-list wildcard retaining woody behavior; zero probability must still mean no overtopping. Use typed species names, serde defaults and a registry entry. Test allowed velvetpad, forbidden stonecushion/springturf/non-cover, an armed site's target changing, gates closing, seed loss, and unchanged woody replacement. Retain normal funded-seed spending and the old stand's booked death. Update the comments/tests that currently equate “positive overtop” with “woody”.

3. **Important — Runners: seeds only first is appropriate, but measure dispersal and canopy failure separately from probability.** Evidence: `crates/cubarium-voxel-flora/src/lib.rs:1741–1763`; `crates/cubarium-voxel-flora/src/seeds.rs:804–848,900–930`; `design/velvetpad-niche-r2-2026-10-03.md:61–67,84–97`. Springturf's raw terrain sky gate is **0.75**, not the diagnostic's generic “lit” boundary of 0.6. Its shallow root box must have water ≥0.3, saturated fraction ≤0.3 and standing depth ≤0.03 m. Open terrain can still be canopy shaded: an adult needs roughly raw effective light L≥0.5 under the study's reference conditions, and a gate-passing seed can therefore replace a healthy pad with a failing juvenile. The reference hop is 3 (resolved to 6 voxels at 0.125 m), versus velvetpad spores' resolved hop 12. Drop dispersal samples the highest support in a nearby column; it is not guaranteed to deliver to every nearby mat. Increasing p cannot fix absent banks or inaccessible surfaces. **Change:** retain current hop, clonal share 0.3 and vacant-only runners in the first arm. Classify no-arrival, failed-gate, failed-arm and post-birth losses, including distance to turf donors. If front advance is seed-limited, study target-restricted runners separately, with paid packages, target checks and booked replacement; do not silently let them bypass the seed experiment's delay or probability.

4. **Important — Reversibility and facilitation: permit succession, do not promise a mandatory sequence.** Evidence: `crates/cubarium-voxel-flora/src/seeds.rs:399–425,468–475,804–848`; proposal `:56–79`. Woody plants can replace velvetpad directly today, and springturf can establish bare ground without moss. They can also replace turf before it dominates. The proposal therefore adds one succession route, not three compulsory stages. Velvetpad cannot invade living turf, but can recolonize suitable vacancies after mortality; grazing normally crops foliage above the refuge rather than directly clearing a stand (`crates/cubarium-voxel-flora/src/lib.rs:1029–1031,1774–1775`). Moisture/light refuges are plausible, not an entitlement to every shaded or soggy site: today's velvetpad itself excludes wholly saturated roots and excessive flooding. **Change:** keep replacement one-way while turf lives; do not add reverse overtopping. Test recovery after an explicitly booked disturbance and distinguish cropping from actual stand death. Use the plain draw initially: no age or litter gate. If Wrysk later wants obligatory pioneer facilitation, name that as an additional model change. Litter produced by killing the old mat is already a consequence of replacement, but is not evidence that moss was necessary to establish the successor.

5. **Important — Correct the production premise: a turf site carries much less foliage and sheds less absolute litter than an adult pad.** Evidence: `config/desktop/terrarium.toml:196–207,248–259`; `crates/cubarium-voxel-flora/src/lib.rs:1764–1775,1948–1958`; proposal `:35–38,80–83`. The proposal compares springturf's **default** foliage rate 0.02 to velvetpad's **preset** rate 0.0061. Resolved turf is **0.006785**, only about 1.11× the rate coefficient. At adult wood maxima, foliage capacity is `1.285276 × 0.06 = 0.0771 ou` for turf versus `4 × 0.2 = 0.8 ou` for velvetpad. Both have refuge 0.3: nominal edible capacities are **0.054 versus 0.56 ou**. Full-foliage shedding before the shared night multiplier is **0.000120 versus 0.000465 ou/s**, so turf sheds about **26% as much per adult site**, despite its higher fractional senescence. Maximum foliage construction requests scale with wood too: approximately **0.000407 versus 0.001221 ou/s**. These are capacity/reference comparisons, not measured sustained production, but they invalidate “foliage and litter should hold or rise” from stand replacement alone. **Change:** correct the resolved numbers and treat food replacement as an experimental question. Measure edible mass, regrowth and litter input per area and per world. Do not increase turf mortality, biomass or assimilation in the same first arm to hide an overtopping failure. If succession works but food falls, bring a separate turf production/body-capacity adjustment to the owner.

7. **Important — Fauna: mature plants plus a matched introduction is necessary, but misses the transition and can benefit from a one-time litter pulse.** Evidence: proposal `:80–83`; `crates/cubarium-voxel-flora/src/seeds.rs:379–380,468–471`; `crates/cubarium/examples/velvet_niche.rs:574–598,640–661`. The study harness's minute-sampled death inference remains unsuitable for exact cause counts (`velvet_niche.rs:546–566`); use actual replacement events and population closure.
