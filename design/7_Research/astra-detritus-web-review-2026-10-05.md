# Astra's review: lanternjaw and detritus-web proposal

Reviewer: Astra (gpt-6-astra via the codex companion). Subject:
`design/lanternjaw-detritus-web-proposal-2026-10-05.md` (7107e45b). This is evidence, not a decision.

| Proposal item | Verdict | Concrete finding / required change |
|---|---|---|
| **§3.0: microbes hold litter; shredders follow it** | **accept-with-changes** | First-order decay supplies negative feedback: more litter → more removal ([flora/step.rs:3487–3538](/home/wrysk/wryskware/cubarium/crates/cubarium-voxel-flora/src/step.rs:3487)). Bounded litter still requires bounded plant input. Already-saturated mouths will not breed faster merely because total litter rises; measure **occupied food area and recruitment**, not just litter mass. |
| **§3.1A: stay on food** | **accept-with-changes** | Fixes the documented full-speed feeding ([controller.rs:674–684](/home/wrysk/wryskware/cubarium/crates/cubarium-voxel-fauna/src/controller.rs:674)). Preserve escape priority and leave exhausted patches. Delete “none for stability”: concentrated feeding can strip film/caps, and stationary shredders generate less predator-triggering footfall. |
| **§3.1B: dark-active** | **accept** | A useful feeding opportunity and spatial refuge, not population regulation by itself. Open-map recruitment must still replace deaths. For snails, darkness increases feeding opportunity while reducing film production; assess that combination separately. |
| **§3.1C: earlier breeding, smaller young, surplus clutches** | **accept-with-changes** | Eligibility currently requires laying size plus package-and-floor reserve ([fauna/step.rs:2781–2787](/home/wrysk/wryskware/cubarium/crates/cubarium-voxel-fauna/src/step.rs:2781), [fauna/lib.rs:1518–1545](/home/wrysk/wryskware/cubarium/crates/cubarium-voxel-fauna/src/lib.rs:1518)). Gate on **one affordable egg**, calculate actual clutch at laying, and permit zero when unaffordable. Otherwise a maximum-clutch eligibility gate defeats surplus breeding. |
| **§3.1C / §3.4: “within about a world day”** | **reject** | Its own arithmetic gives `ln(0.4/0.12)/g`: **2.7–4 days = 5.4–8 h**, before reserve filling, hold and incubation. At unchanged measured growth, maturation remains **12–25 days**, beyond the six-day life. Already-mature founders can respond quickly; newborn generations cannot. Demonstrate faster intake and surviving replacement offspring. |
| **§3.1D: nest-site limitation** | **accept-with-changes** | Spacing limits simultaneous clutches, not adults. Eggs hatch in roughly **3 minutes**, versus an **18-minute** laying interval (§1.2); sites reopen quickly ([fauna/step.rs:3230–3274](/home/wrysk/wryskware/cubarium/crates/cubarium-voxel-fauna/src/step.rs:3230)). Make site occupancy reflect local adults/juveniles or persist through recruitment. Verify suppression actually precedes food shortage. |
| **§3.1D: egg cannibalism** | **accept-with-changes** | Start with own-lineage eggs. Cross-lineage consumption lets abundant shredders erase rare capgnawers, releasing glowcap. Egg encounters must fall sufficiently at low density to permit replacement; staying motionless on litter may reduce those encounters. Ordinary feeding currently has no clutch source ([fauna/step.rs:2516–2553](/home/wrysk/wryskware/cubarium/crates/cubarium-voxel-fauna/src/step.rs:2516)). |
| **§3.1E: capgnawers hold glowcap** | **accept-with-changes** | **Caps eaten → stands thin is unproven.** Fungal intake scales with mycelial wood, not caps ([flora/step.rs:2206](/home/wrysk/wryskware/cubarium/crates/cubarium-voxel-flora/src/step.rs:2206)); dark seep also supplies income independently of litter ([flora/step.rs:2937–2953](/home/wrysk/wryskware/cubarium/crates/cubarium-voxel-flora/src/step.rs:2937)). Show grazing reduces spore recruitment, mycelial growth or survival. Fungal egg sites need a new rule; current laying supports litter/film only ([fauna/step.rs:3092–3097](/home/wrysk/wryskware/cubarium/crates/cubarium-voxel-fauna/src/step.rs:3092)). |
| **§3.1E: defence and litter fallback damp crashes** | **accept-with-changes** | Defence protects caps; litter supports capgnawers ([body.rs:1238–1250](/home/wrysk/wryskware/cubarium/crates/cubarium-voxel-fauna/src/body.rs:1238)). Neither guarantees coexistence: abundant fallback can sustain grazing pressure when fungus becomes rare. Require cap preference when abundant, fallback searching when scarce, and recovery from the fungal refuge. |
| **§3.1F: film alone holds fast-breeding snails** | **accept-with-changes** | Highest direct food-collapse risk. Film grows proportionally to remaining film ([film.rs:117–124](/home/wrysk/wryskware/cubarium/crates/cubarium-voxel-flora/src/film.rs:117)); grazing can remove the entire pool ([flora/lib.rs:5453–5459](/home/wrysk/wryskware/cubarium/crates/cubarium-voxel-flora/src/lib.rs:5453)). Keep inaccessible film refuges and apply recruitment crowding to snails too. Logistic growth alone does not prevent extinction. |
| **§3.2: patrol, short waits, tremor, lure** | **accept-with-changes** | Corrects crown seeking ([controller.rs:1345–1368](/home/wrysk/wryskware/cubarium/crates/cubarium-voxel-fauna/src/controller.rs:1345)). Food cues indicate potential habitat, not actual killable prey. Add fruitless-patch memory/exploration and condition prey selection on the hunter’s size window ([body.rs:826–843](/home/wrysk/wryskware/cubarium/crates/cubarium-voxel-fauna/src/body.rs:826)). |
| **§3.2: switching guarantees rare-prey refuge** | **reject** | Choosing the largest recent catch share does not establish a type III response or prevent extinction. Catch memory can reinforce yesterday’s prey preference; rare prey still receive opportunistic strikes. Add encounter-based updates, exploration and reduced attack preference as eligible prey become scarce; demonstrate falling **per-prey mortality** at low abundance. |
| **§3.2 / §3.4: higher upkeep, reserve cap 0.5–0.6** | **reject** | **This breaks sustained breeding.** The retained three eggs cost **0.45 org**; terrarium floor is **0.2876**, requiring **0.7376 reserve** ([fauna/lib.rs:1501–1527](/home/wrysk/wryskware/cubarium/crates/cubarium-voxel-fauna/src/lib.rs:1501), [terrarium.toml:292–296](/home/wrysk/wryskware/cubarium/config/desktop/terrarium.toml:292)). Adult body is **1.05**, so proposed intake capacity is only **0.525–0.630**. Revise package/floor coherently before changing capacity. Higher upkeep creates demand, not recruitment. |
| **§3.2: retained footfall → parasite → adult → egg-bank loop** | **accept-with-changes** | Hatching requires dusk, traffic, no nearby adult **and a host** ([fauna/step.rs:3312–3394](/home/wrysk/wryskware/cubarium/crates/cubarium-voxel-fauna/src/step.rs:3312)). At one-third per eligible dusk, mean waiting is three days; host/suppression add delay. Small hosts supply less growth: adding ~0.15 body at `0.09 × host-body/day` takes **≥4.4 days on a full shredder**, ignoring rider costs ([fauna/step.rs:441–489](/home/wrysk/wryskware/cubarium/crates/cubarium-voxel-fauna/src/step.rs:441)). Verify the whole chain; eggs expire and cannot guarantee recovery. |
| **§3.3: missing predators, comminution; §3.5: body ceiling** | **accept-with-changes** | Bellwing’s missing consumer is real ([body.rs:829–833](/home/wrysk/wryskware/cubarium/crates/cubarium-voxel-fauna/src/body.rs:829)); handle separately. A snail predator is optional once film refuges/crowding work. Comminution is optional nutrient turnover. A body ceiling is acceptable only as a labelled compute guard; repeated activation means ecological brakes failed. |

**Build order:** instrumentation and breeding-affordability checks → A/B feeding behaviour → density brake D → C fast reproduction → E fungal recruitment response and F film refuges → lanternjaw sensing/patrol → switching → coherent metabolism/reproduction changes. Ship reproduction with its brake; retain separate experimental arms to identify each effect.

**Patch, Tachyon-size (§4):** branch identical saves into baseline, A/B, A/B+C+D, and brake-removal arms. Ensure every reviewed lineage is present: §2.1 reports capgnawers on only two patch seeds. Measure intake, awake/access shares, hatch-to-first-clutch, offspring surviving to breed, recruitment/adult versus local density, egg survival/refusals, and crowding onset versus food shortage. **Replacement**, not merely births, closes the consumer loop.

**Glowcap/snail measurements:** caps grazed versus regrowth; fungal wood, spore establishment and stand deaths; litter/wood uptake and seep input; film minimum, ungrazed refuge area and recolonisation. Record capgnawer fallback share. A flat stand count caused by exhausted space does not demonstrate grazer regulation.

**Terrarium:** compare the same prey web with/without lanternjaws. Track egg-bank age/viability, footfall, host availability/size, attachment → drop-off → free adult → laying times, and bank replenishment. Measure attacks/kills against **locally available, size-eligible prey**, per-prey mortality at low density, grazer collateral kills and Veilhound losses.

**Timing and success (§4):** use eight seeds × staged 2 h for immediate mechanism response, checking movement, eating and conservation after every load. Require counters only when their triggering conditions occur. Branch longer observations from relevant ages: a 6–8 h soak cannot establish six-day/12 h prey replacement or twelve-day/24 h predator replacement. Judge bounded cycles and viable recovery across seeds; declining 2 h CV alone can hide extinction. Log minimum living/recruitable populations and ms/tick.
