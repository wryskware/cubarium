# Two-hour quiet diagnostic: rare recovery, mixed biology

**Keep Off; stop automatic escalation of this family.** The unchanged candidate
works as a rare paid-birth recovery gesture, not an everyday quiet habit. The
two-hour evidence does **not** confirm a growing pooled birth deficit or ecological
collapse. It does retain a small adverse no-care population-time tendency and
heterogeneous seed outcomes, without establishing a sufficiently frequent behavioral
benefit. Do not deploy, lengthen the timer, relax affordability or launch24/72-hour
runs from this result. A separately designed post-feeding mechanism is the more
direct next question; no new rule or experiment is selected in this report.

This is noncanonical interpretation of all twelve seeds/all48 arms. No reducer,
core, recipe, art, original artifact, live state or process was changed.

## What the behavior supplies over two hours

No-care: **1,239 bouts across997 distinct parents**, all40 ticks, all1,239 released;
3,442 unaffordable refusals out of4,681 births (26.47% admitted). No abort or
held-interval death. Actual Begin records carry **334 Seeking/905 Feeding**, zero
Resting. Thus these admissions follow actual active modes, predominantly the
already-low-effort feeding mode—not evidence that every subsequent ordinary
counterfactual tick would have been active. Existing upkeep/growth/oxidation remain
paid. Actual total transported recovery distance is35.437 px across twelve worlds;
this is reduced effort with real residual movement, not a position freeze.

Recovery occupies49,560 living-organism ticks: **0.029794% of living time**, nearly
unchanged from ten minutes'0.03048%. Merging overlapping bouts gives2,427.95 world
seconds with any recovery across86,400 world seconds: **2.8101%**, versus2.903% in
the short screen. Individual no-care seeds have79–150 bouts (roughly one per48–91 s
of world time); individual living-time shares0.02221–0.04141%. The pooled event
spacing is69.7 s/world. Opportunity exists in12/12 seeds, but an unconditioned
whole-world glance has no recovery~97.2% of the time, before face visibility or
occlusion. The proposed9/12 screen is met; everyday visible prevalence is not.

Fed:1,365 admissions across1,087 parents,362 Seeking/1,003 Feeding,3,367
unaffordable refusals;1,363 observed releases and **two censored bouts** (seed3:
15 observed ticks; seed11:13), not two failed releases.54,548 held ticks,
0.032699% living time,3.0982% world-time coverage,38.834 px residual path. No abort
or held-interval death. Natural satiated rest remains **zero in all48 arms**.
No native-render or physical-display evidence is furnished by this biological run.

## Diversity, recruitment and attribution limits

No total extinction, **no matched reference-only form** in any pair. All no-care
closing form sets match. Fed seed1 retains form3 only in candidate (a benefit);
elsewhere fed sets match. Some initially present form3 populations disappear in
both arms (seed3; additionally seed5 fed), not a candidate-only loss.

No-care ancestry turnover totals **126 Off-only /139 candidate-only**; fed120/133.
Both directions occur in every seed. Net richness improves by13 in each condition,
but the identical net result hides different identities. Such turnover is not
automatically global harm; preserving every exact ancestry is not the owner's
objective. Likewise net richness is not proof of preserved genetic diversity.

Every opening animal has died by close in all arms; closing populations are actual
run-born descendants, so this horizon supplies real multigeneration recruitment
evidence absent at ten minutes. Of run-born animals,2,297→2,288 no-care and
2,282→2,328 fed actually produce a further paid child within the run. Among children
whose own birth triggered candidate recovery,585 no-care/662 fed themselves reproduce;
291/347 remain alive at close. These are actual achievements, not maturity inferred
from age—but they are **not exposure-matched fecundity probabilities**. Birth timing,
death and right censoring differ; no causal benefit to those children is established.
Closing run-born ages span14–142,528 ticks in candidate no-care,13–142,765 fed.

Post-step living-time totals omit the terminal dead organism's within-step fraction;
the observer's separate held-death fields prevent silently misclassifying a final
held interval. Here those fields are zero, so recovery denominators have no such
dead-interval correction. General population/intake/path comparisons retain the
post-step survivor scope. Exact ordinary funding, oxidation, missed intake amounts
and individual causal debits remain unsupported; no post-step stock subtraction is
substituted for mutation-site accounting.

## Disposition and reproduction

Close this fixed candidate's current evidence loop as **functionally correct,
behaviorally sparse, biologically mixed**. Reject it as the demonstrated solution
to everyday quiet; do not claim proven ecological unviability. Keep it available as
an opt-in research mechanism/rare birth gesture, with Off unchanged. Additional long
runs of the same timer are not the next useful test of frequency. Separately scope
an affordable, explicitly rearmed genuine post-feeding episode using actual intake
and current stocks, retaining unchanged costs and a bounded missed-feeding duty.
The art-side sail study remains separate and cannot manufacture physiological quiet.
