## Reading

- Nutrient diffusion had no effect because nutrient never limited producer
  growth: with `N = 1 m` per cell and the `f_max · N` cap far above realized
  growth, the recycling loop was inert. The spec now uses a Monod term
  `N/(N + K_N)` with scarcer initial nutrient.
- Every viable configuration settled with the minimum producer stock at about
  0.105 of `P_max`, i.e. the `feed_min` refuge floor, everywhere: a uniformly
  grazed lawn whose population is set by regrowth from that floor. Population
  CV of 0.02–0.07 and negative face synchrony mean no whole-cube cycles, but
  also no visible local succession at the whole-population level; the spatial
  metrics needed to judge succession were not yet recorded.
- Slow growth (0.004/s) with slow movement produced boom–bust cycles with
  extinction in 7 of 8 seeds; with fast movement it cycled without extinction.
- Moving versus static weather made little difference at this stage.
- Mass residuals stayed below 2.3e-9 over 432,000 ticks (rounding accumulation
  of about 1e-12 relative).

## Limits

Six hours per run, four seeds, one genotype, no mutation or predation. The
population-level metrics cannot distinguish a patchy world from a uniform one;
the second batch adds field dumps and spatial metrics.
