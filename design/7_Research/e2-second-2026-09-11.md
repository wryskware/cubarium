## Reading

- Nutrient limitation now acts (initial nutrient changes populations and
  slow-growth worlds crash), but the spatial picture is unchanged: the fraction
  of cells at or below a quarter of `P_max` is 0.9–1.0 in every configuration,
  no cell reaches half of `P_max` after burn-in, the producer correlation
  length is one to two cells, and the spatial coefficient of variation is about
  0.1. Every cell is held at the `feed_min` floor: a uniformly grazed lawn.
- Populations are stable and unsynchronized at growth 0.016 (CV 0.04–0.08,
  face synchrony negative), so the whole-cube crash mode is absent there; slow
  growth with slow movement produces synchronized crashes (face synchrony 0.7,
  15–21 crash cycles, one or two extinctions per configuration).
- Interpretation: a single mobile generalist with linear intake strips any cell
  above the threshold as soon as it is found; the population then settles where
  regrowth from the floor balances intake. Nothing in the current design makes
  poor cells poor food, makes regrowth strongly patchy, or keeps consumers
  local, so no local succession can exist. E2's gate is not met.

## Response adopted for the third batch

Saturating intake `q ∝ P/(P + K_P)`, stronger static habitat contrast, larger
slow weather amplitude, and shorter sensing, tested as axes rather than assumed.
